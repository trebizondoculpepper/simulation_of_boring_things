# Resynthesised bloops

Rebuilt on 2026-10-27 from the owner's own console sounds in `../science_fiction_bleeps/` (same file names), for the owner to compare and choose between. Nothing here is a sample of the originals: each is regenerated by `resynth.py`.

Method: each original is decoded to mono 48 kHz and analysed with a short-time Fourier transform (2048-point Blackman–Harris window, 5 ms hop). Up to six spectral peaks a frame above −60 dB are picked and linked into partial tracks (a peak continues a track within 6 % in frequency; fragments under 15 ms are dropped). Each track is rebuilt by a phase-continuous sine oscillator following its frequency and amplitude, faded in and out over a frame. What the sines do not explain is kept only as its coarse spectral envelope (smoothed across frequency and time) and rebuilt from white noise at 0.7 of its level, so hiss and clicks keep their character. Each result is scaled to its original's peak level, given 3 ms end fades, and encoded as MP3 (VBR q2).

Rerun: `python3 -I resynth.py ../science_fiction_bleeps .` from this folder.
