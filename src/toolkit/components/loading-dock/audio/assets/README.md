# Audio asset record

`shutter-run.wav` is 52.0–60.5 s of nicola_ariutti's CC0 Freesound recording of an electric roller shutter (sound 335364), downloaded by the owner's account on 2026-10-26: its second take, the shutter running steadily, mixed to mono, band-passed 40 Hz to 9 kHz, resampled to 22,050 Hz, its last half second crossfaded into its first to make a seamless 8-second loop, peak-normalised to 0.6 and saved as PCM16 WAV. The motor's hum (peaks near 85, 160, 260, 390 and a gear whine near 650 Hz) and the laths' clanks (8 to 11 a second, each over in a millisecond or two) are both in it. The dev43 shutter holds it at level 0.6 while it runs.
