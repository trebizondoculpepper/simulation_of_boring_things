# PR_A elbow corridor and URL positions

The owner asked to reset PR_A after trying an east elbow, stair, alcove and lower hall. Those geometry components and their collision and floor elevation rules were removed. PR_A again starts at its original position and has its closed east wall and glazing. The next shape will be specified by the owner, including whether it is an extension or an adjacent room and whether it is wet or dry.

Room URLs accept x, y, z, yaw and pitch after the room ID. Coordinates are metres and angles degrees; y is camera height. A global Copy location button shows the current room name and copies a matching URL. The overlay is attached to the document body so room canvases cannot hide it.
