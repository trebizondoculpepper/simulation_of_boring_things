# Stepped room north-wall alignment

The west extension of `CR_A_STEPS` ended 2.8 m south of the main room's window wall. The owner identified the two walls from positions `(-8.88, 26.00)` and `(-5.89, 25.12)` and asked for one flush north-wall plane.

The extension's north limit now equals the main room's north limit. Its floor, ceiling, north and west walls, and collision footprint follow the same enlarged outline. The full-height return wall was removed. Tiled side panels close the structure beneath the upper landing and each step, preserving the stair-side barrier and hiding the void that appeared when the lower room was first extended.

Both supplied camera views were checked in Chrome. The extension wall and window wall now align, and the stair-side lining is visible from below.
