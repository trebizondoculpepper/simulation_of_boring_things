# Jacuzzi fibre illumination

The Dev30 cast-concrete fibre booth now has a white/rainbow selector. Its pinpoint source is two screen pixels wide without distance attenuation, with a high-contrast illuminator-wheel pulse; the colour wheel gives fibres different phases and a slow hue drift.

Pool 03 and the Dev35 Jacuzzi prototype place fibre ends directly on the bare concrete above the tile line. The production hall reads the illuminator state from the saved world. A Penfold control box on the north wall of plant room 3, beside the water-circuit mimic, writes on/off, brightness, twinkle speed and colour selection to that state. The running lamp reads it back. Speed zero removes the twinkle pulse.

Browser checks showed the white points on Pool 03's concrete and the control box clear of the existing mimic. The Dev30 selector produced coloured points when worked. Pure state, world save and architectural lighting tests cover the controls and pulse.
