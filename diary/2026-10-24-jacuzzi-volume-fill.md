# Jacuzzi fill by volume

The owner approved the jacuzzi's three-sided plinth and the effects panel's short post, then asked for simulated water quantity to be the source of truth, with percent full and visible height derived from that quantity. The tub now uses two tapered circular basins with a wider upper basin across the seat. The seat lies at approximately 20 % of the tub's capacity. A general pure basin-profile utility converts water volume to percent and height, and the stepped-tub profile supplies its volume curve and surface radius. Tests cover empty/full endpoints, the seat transition and volume conservation. Dev35's control is labelled FILL % to distinguish its command from a height setting. The post was moved to x=-0.4 m, z=-0.04 m, facing the supplied viewpoint with the tub beyond it; its collision footprint moved with it.

The existing main-world pools still store water surface level in their saved state. Migrating them to volume as the only dynamic water quantity requires a world-version migration, volume curves for their different basin footprints, and replacing level-based flow and draining calculations. The general basin-profile interface is the first reusable piece; this session changes the jacuzzi demonstration's fill behaviour.
