# Engineer stores cabinets

The Dev1 cupboard model now has a fixed fascia that carries a configurable cabinet label and an Ironside maker badge. This keeps the identity visible with either the steel or glazed doors open.

The cupboards' doors act as pairs, and drawers can only operate after they open. Closing a cupboard with drawers out waits briefly, retracts the drawers, and then shuts both leaves. Drawers and the other storage containers have individual IDs and changeable front labels. Each drawer contains small removable objects that use the shared carrying controls; objects can be returned to their original open drawer.
