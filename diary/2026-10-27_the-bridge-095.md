## Problem Statement

Mission 095: make dev46's starship bridge the game's control room, reached from the main corridor's control room door, the old control room retired but kept; wire dev48's reimagined panels to the one world state exactly as their originals are, local-only controls refused; build Central Control from what the model has; give the big wall a live viewscreen. ## Our Approach

Keep the bridge on dev46's shared builder (`views/control-room-prototype.ts`) with a `game` option, so the prototypes and the game room share one geometry, and run it in the shell room runner with its doorway joined to the corridor's. Wire the panels by data: a binding per panel (`logic/bridge-bindings.ts`) that gives each lamp, read-out, gauge, selector, button, symbol, banner, list row and annunciator window its read of the world and each press its command, reusing the originals' own reads (`mainPoolState`, `heatingState`, `airState`, the pump panel's starters, `loungeFilterMimicOf`) and their actions; refusals (`local`, `unmodelled`) are commands too, so the panel answers them with a deny bleep and the reason. A test pins every item of every wired panel as wired, and each panel to its original's node in its room's model.
