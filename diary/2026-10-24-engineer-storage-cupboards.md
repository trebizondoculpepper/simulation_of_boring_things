# Engineer's storage cupboards

The owner supplied a photograph of steel stores cupboards and chose both solid and glazed door versions, including the small parts drawers. I built one reusable 0.96 × 0.50 × 1.98 m model with a door-style option and placed two instances at the right end of Dev1's north-wall rack lineup. Each has hinged doors, nine sliding translucent parts drawers with pull and label pockets, five yellow parts bins, a blue bin, shelves, archive boxes, a tool chest and cases. The models are room dressing intended for later use in the engineer's office; they have no plant simulation state.

The gallery's existing Use control operates doors and drawers. I checked the view in the browser and worked the middle drawer of the solid-door cupboard; it slid out in view. The two cabinets fit beside the existing rack lineup without occupying the plinth aisles.
