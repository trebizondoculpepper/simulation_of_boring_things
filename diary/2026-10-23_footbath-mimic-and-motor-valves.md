# Footbath cabinet indication and motor valves

The packaged footbath cabinet already had a small circuit mimic and water-temperature LED readout on its left door. Its drain and make-up LEDs were too small to read from the aisle. I added larger DRAINING and FILLING annunciators below the circuit drawing. DRAINING now requires the drain/return valve line-up, cabinet pump power and water above the footbath floor; FILLING requires the make-up valve open, the tank float calling and a live wet hall booster. The small make-up LED still reports valve position. The temperature comes from the saved pool world state, and its +/− buttons change the setpoint on the first press.

The footbath make-up main runs south to north. The duplicate pipe plate on the far side had kept the same arrow despite facing the other way; far-side plates now reverse their printed arrow. The owner then replaced the foot spa's three named hand valves with motorised valves and a wall panel. The same motor and panel treatment now covers the other fitted water and heating valves in that room. The wider, higher panel has two valves per row, each with red SHUT and green OPEN illuminated pushbuttons. The old two-position selector required clicking the correct half of a very small knob and could seem unable to close. The cabinet pump now cuts out and lights LOW WATER if its balance tank trips or the footbath empties while draining; changing to the return line-up permits refill.

The proposed small, clear make-up jets and surface ripples were written as `doc/prompts/done/041-footspa-makeup-jets.md` at the owner's request, to be built later.
