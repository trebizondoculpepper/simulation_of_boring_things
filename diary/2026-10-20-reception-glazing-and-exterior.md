# Reception glazing and exterior

Opened the reception east wall onto a paved forecourt and replaced its east and south wall finishes with framed glass. Added a simple walkable exterior setting with grass, pavement, a road, neighbouring low buildings, trees, canopy and site lights. Copied Encabulator’s procedural sky dome and sky presets, using FLUFFY CLOUDS by default. The source repository was read only; copied modules are recorded in the reuse inventory.
