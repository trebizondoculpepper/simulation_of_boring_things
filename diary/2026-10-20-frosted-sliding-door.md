# Frosted reception entrance

Changed the reception-to-changing doorway from hinged opaque leaves to two self-closing sliding glass leaves. The glass uses rough transmission so the actual changing village appears blurred behind it, with slim metal borders, a top track and floor guide. This one neighbouring room stays loaded while the door is shut to support the blurred view; other opaque doors still unload their destinations.

Checked the closed glass from reception and walked through the open sliding entrance in the browser. Typecheck, lint, formatting check, tests and build passed.
