# The bridge’s ambient occlusion

The owner asked why the bridge rendered slowly and then asked for a cheaper way to retain ambient occlusion. After pulling `110913d`, profiled the live bridge in an isolated Chrome with ANGLE Metal. Its GTAO pass redraws the scene and was the dominant measured effect cost at a 2560 × 1426 render size. Six AO samples preserved the visible contact shading while improving the short measured run from about 47 to 58 fps; four samples did not improve it further. Applied six samples only to the bridge. Details and limits are in `doc/reports/bridge-render-performance-2026-10-28.md`. The user’s existing uncommitted truck-yard work was untouched.

Validation: typecheck, lint (existing warnings), format check and build 0.1.660 passed. The full suite reported 3,010 passing tests, 10 skipped and one 5 s Wash-O-Tron AUTO timing timeout under concurrent load; all seven Wash-O-Tron tests passed on a focused rerun (2.82 s). The existing parallel timing follow-up remains open in TODO. Visual comparison was in isolated Chrome at the bridge stair landing; browser acceptance on other GPUs remains open.
