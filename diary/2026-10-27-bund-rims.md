# Chemical bund rim z-fighting

The red/yellow caps on dose-bottle spill pallets ended at the same height as the black walls, and neighbouring cap boxes overlapped at their corners. Shortened the black walls to the underside of the caps; the front/back caps span the rim while the side caps fit between them. The caps retain a 1 mm overhang and the existing overall height, footprint and sump capacity. Rim height is bounded for very small bottles. No model or interaction changes.

Validation: typecheck, lint (existing warnings only), formatting and build 0.1.555 pass. A full run overlapped a separate chemical-distribution update and saw two power assertions fail; those tests pass on the completed update. Repeated the full suite in a fixed checkout at `edbaa6a` with this geometry patch: 2,534 passed, 10 skipped (four workers). No browser visual acceptance was performed.
