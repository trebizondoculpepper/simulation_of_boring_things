# Empty pools and first fittings

Three dry basins now cut through the Main Pool Hall floor: a 50 × 20 m main basin at 1.8 m depth, an 11 × 8 m learner basin at 0.9 m, and a 9 × 8 m spa basin at 1.1 m. Each has tiled interior, pale coping and a narrow overflow strip. The main basin has simple lane stripes. The first-person collision keeps the player on the deck. Two original simplified ladders and repeated ceiling light fittings establish a first furnishing pass. The rapids area remains unbuilt while its shape is decided.

`doc/research/pool-architectural-fittings.md` records manufacturer examples and the asset-use boundary. No external BIM models or textures were imported. The lights are visual fittings under the existing room illumination, without photometric simulation. Pool hydraulics and machinery remain unimplemented.
