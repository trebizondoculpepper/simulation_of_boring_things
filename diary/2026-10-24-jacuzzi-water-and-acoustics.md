# Jacuzzi water and approach acoustics

Wired the live tub's visible water quantity to the world's jacuzzi fill fraction, adapting the simulation's mean-depth quantity to the existing stepped-basin volume profile. The existing water height, width, wet/dry visibility, jets and filling arcs now follow that quantity. Development-room effects still use their private fill target. A real-component regression builds the live tub against a mock world store and checks full, below-seat, empty and refilled states.

The owner's music requirement is a route-based approach fade, reaching nominal amplifier volume before entry from either side. Added a pure distance calculation using each corridor's laid-out pieces and the existing coordinate handover. The Olympic stairs and the rotunda approach share the same smooth curve: silent at 12 m, nominal level in the final metre, and no further attenuation inside the jacuzzi hall. The app owns one listener-relative music stream across room changes; physical hall speakers remain in place but no longer add a second distance-attenuated stream. The isolated development monitor and existing plant-room through-wall music remain separate. Tests verify corridor-copy invariance, increasing level down the stairs and toward the hall, and that gain changes do not restart playback.

The owner also requested −30 dB drain noise in the jacuzzi with its plant door shut. A hall-side receiver follows actual world discharge and uses an exact 10^(-30/20) amplitude multiplier at zero door angle, smoothly opening to nominal transmission over 0.35 radians. Hidden/following plant scenes now stop their direct cabinet drain and motor sounds, avoiding a full-volume leak alongside the attenuated hall receiver. A shared drain recipe now supplies the jacuzzi, footbath and Dev23 gully demonstration; no new recording was introduced.
