# Plant-room loading profile

Investigated the owner's 1–3 second doorway delay and saved `doc/reports/plant-room-load-performance-2026-10-24.md` with raw production timing samples in `doc/reports/data/plant-room-load-2026-10-24.json`. Temporary source timers separated module readiness, construction, equipment stages and the first composer frame. Ran both development checks and a production preview; the report uses the latter for its main comparison. Hardware reported Apple M5 through ANGLE Metal.

Plant doors are marked `onOpen` and excluded from nearest-room preloading, so preparation starts after opening. In an actual jacuzzi doorway trial the module became ready in 50 ms, construction took 315 ms and the first composer frame 5,722 ms; it created 29 programs. Direct Olympic construction took 1,632 ms, of which equipment was 922 ms, while its first frame took 5,593 ms. Its ozone-generator factory subtree was 768 ms, whereas pure ozone model creation in Node was under 1 ms. The report distinguishes measured wall time from inferred shader/driver/upload costs and notes cache and browser-session limitations.

Recommendations prioritize prefetching plant rooms on approach, warming the full scene/composer, a bounded recent-room cache, then static geometry/material batching and profiling expensive component factories. The previous clipping-plane-count fix is already present. Removed every temporary source profiling hook; no performance optimization was implemented.
