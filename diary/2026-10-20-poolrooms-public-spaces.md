# Poolrooms public spaces

Changed the default sky to overcast, reduced outdoor illumination and added local sources beneath hall, changing and reception luminaires. Public floors, walls and basins now use pale glossy ceramic materials with visible grout.

The empty basins have slatted perimeter overflow channels and floor outlet grilles. The changing village has cubicles, lockers, benches, vanities and showers. Reception has a counter, glazed screen, waiting bench and entry rails. Furniture is part of each room model; large footprints join first person collision. Reference choices are recorded in `doc/research/poolrooms-public-spaces.md`.

Checked the hall and reception at walking height in the browser. Typecheck, lint, formatting check, tests and build passed.
