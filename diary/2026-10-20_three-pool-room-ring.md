# Three pool room ring

The public-facing room index now starts with only three pool spaces: existing dev11, a Captain’s Quarters-inspired winding shallow pool, and a Pike-inspired tiled basin with a flooded passage. Older plant studies remain under an expandable heading, and equipment development rooms remain available for testing.

The Captain’s Quarters room uses the two local reference images for its winding channel, 0.9144 m (3 ft) water depth, broad coping, a raised observation deck with six walkable treads, blue cross beams, repeated columns, glazing and a tiled pavilion. The Pike room uses the five local Jared Pike references for an angular basin, tiled spit, submerged passage, low coffered ceiling, round columns, bright apertures and warm/dark end-wall recesses. Both rooms reuse dev11’s procedural tile material and the common first-person controls. They are geometry and atmosphere studies; water is a simple transparent surface, and no plant is attached.

Each pool room has two spring-door portals to the other two rooms. Dev11 is no longer part of the development-corridor portal chain. The web page links directly to all three. The ring topology and room floor geometry have pure tests. Typecheck, lint, full tests and build pass; the new rooms were visually opened in the browser.
