# Fibre optics in concrete: Dev30 trial

The owner wanted a scattering of warm-white points in the exposed concrete above Dev35's jacuzzi, then asked to trial the technique in Dev30 first. The new booth uses the existing board-marked concrete material, 88 seeded fibre-end positions, the booth's dimmer and a two-position twinkle wheel. The fibres are represented as tiny points flush with the face; no surface-mounted lamp bodies appear. I reviewed the booth in the browser at `?room=dev30&x=-4.60&y=1.80&z=1.00&yaw=0&pitch=0&zoom=1.000` and raised their apparent size and brightness until they could be seen clearly against the concrete. This remains a Dev30 trial and is not installed in Dev35.

Construction reference: [Concrete Decor, “Let it Shine: Installing Fiber Optic Light Effects”](https://www.concretedecor.net/departments/outdoor-spaces/let-it-shine-installing-fiber-optic-light-effects/). It describes acrylic fibres embedded through concrete, their ends trimmed flush, and a remote illuminator with a wheel for brightness, colour or twinkling effects. The reported nominal fibre diameter is about 1.5 mm; the rendered points are enlarged for visibility in the game.
