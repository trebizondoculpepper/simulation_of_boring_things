# Jacuzzi make-up and fire-collar fixes

The owner reported that PL3’s balance tank did not refill with its new make-up valve open. Reproduced the close/advance/reopen sequence: an effective “make-up shut” result was being written into the old pool makeUp flag, which then acted as a second permanent inhibit even after the installed valve reopened. The previous test compared independently open and closed worlds and never actually reopened a valve. Extended it to reopen, added a saved-stale-flag regression, and checked that the older pool/make-up action works the physical valve. All three regressions failed before the fix. PL3 now derives make-up enable from its installed valve, recovering stale saved flags on the next step; the old command routes through that valve. No saved shape or world version changed.

After the owner said the live tank still did not appear to fill, inspected the running browser’s saved world without modifying it. The supply and booster were available, the make-up valve open and the tank drain shut. Between snapshots the tank rose from 1.91% to 2.52% in ten simulated seconds and the make-up meter advanced, at about 2.83 m³/h (47 L/min). Its sight tube begins 10 cm above the floor, so the early fill is below the visible gauge; water reaches the tube about two simulated minutes from empty. Reported that measured distinction to the owner; did not change the save or artificially fill the tank.

The owner also supplied an image of flickering X-shaped tabs on a pipe penetration’s fire collar. The make-up and collar regressions passed together (28 tests).
