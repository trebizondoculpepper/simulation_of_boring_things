# Ideal foot spa and lounge routing

The owner asked for an isolated foot spa development room with an A4-proportioned ideal pool, ideal water and power sources, a live water quality sign and the existing cabinet. Dev32 now uses one graph node per visible working exhibit and typed, directed ports for mains water, pool water, LTHW, drainage, 230 V power and sign readings. The room starts empty; the test panel opens make-up and drain, the cabinet runs only with water, and its thermostat heats the vessel. Its grey translucent square vessel fills with a blue block, and its LCD shows degrees Celsius and litres. The physical graph edges are routed with the pipe helper. A graph audit, binding test, route coverage test, pipe clash test and fill/heat/drain simulation test pass. I opened the room in Chrome and saw the pool, sign, sources, controls and routed pipes rendering; the visual control layout was raised after inspection.

For Plant Room 1, I traced the two remaining filter inlet/outlet collisions to outlet risers beside their inlet branches. Each outlet now leaves the vessel to its free side before turning toward the overhead header. Room pipe intersection, equipment clearance, valve reachability and pipe-clash tests pass with zero known clashes. I tried the 12 × 10 m compact shell directly; tests found inaccessible pump and valves, a pipe through a filter and a floor channel through the pump set. I restored the 12 × 11 m first trial and recorded the required redesign in the sizing plan.
