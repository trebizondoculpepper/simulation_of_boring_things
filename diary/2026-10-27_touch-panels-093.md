## Problem Statement

Mission 093 stage 1: a 2D touch-panel widget kit in the design language of the MIT LCARS-style libraries, drawn on canvas textures in the 3D world, with a new dev room showing it. The owner first asked for the game's real panels re-imagined as touch screens; while the work was under way he refined it twice: the kit must be a separate, generic layer that could be lifted into another project, and the 3D side must be generic tools for putting 2D controls on surfaces of any shape; the dev room should simply show a variety of controls on a variety of shapes ("can we lay out LCARS things in a 2D space in a 3D world?"), the real panels left for later.

## Our Approach

Read the two MIT libraries (joernweissenborn/lcars's Stylus sources, agent-P's `LCARS_Components.js`) for palette, proportions, the elbow's outline and blink periods, and wrote our own code from them; nothing was copied whole, and the GPL and unlicensed libraries were not touched. The kit, `src/ui2d/`, is a small retained-mode component tree: layout in pixels, paint on demand through dirty flags, presses in its own pixels answered with an action and a sound for the host. The game's side is a touch surface: a black frame of any outline with ui2d screens set coplanar into it.

## Implementation

- `src/ui2d/`: geometry (stack layout, elbow and pill outlines, trend points, fitting a drawing), theme (`BLOCK_THEME`), `UiNode` and `UiRoot`, and the components (elbow, bar, frame, button, indicator, toggle, segmented, label, readout, gauge, trend, banner, list, page stack, diagram); 16 tests.
- `controls/input/touch-point.ts`: the control scheme tells a touch surface where a use landed (uv and point); `plant-room.ts` wraps each use in it.
- `industrial-control/logic/touch-surface.ts` (outlines, inside tests, screens checked against a frame with their bezels, screen grids, uv and plane to pixel, bending) and `views/touch-surface.ts` (the frame extruded from its outline or a bent sheet, the screens coplanar with polygon offset and render order, one `UiRoot` per screen, Antonio loaded once, bleeps).
- `sound/audio/touch-bleeps.ts`: confirm, deny, page, alert, synthesised.
- Dev47: nine surfaces of different shapes with nonsense content (`gallery/views/touch-panel-gallery.ts`), its own layout round four walls (`rooms/plant/touch-panels.ts`).

## Outcomes

- It works: every surface draws, touches light buttons, switch pages, choose selector positions and bleep. Coplanar screens show no z-fighting.
- A first version of real re-imagined panels (PL1-CP1, the lounge mimic, WH-CP1, the annunciator, the changeover with its interlock, the readings, on a trial world) was written and then set aside unpublished when the owner changed the scope; it can be rebuilt on the touch surface for the later stages.
- Gotchas: rounded buttons in tall cells became discs, so a rounded button now keeps at least 1.6:1; an elbow narrower than its column ran out of its rectangle (clamped); a bottom bar's title block ran off the screen (it now reaches up); the curved strip's middle lay in the wall's plane and fought its stripe; captions in a room with its own layout all went to the north or south wall, so wall exhibits' captions now go under them.
