# Prepare the nearest room before opening its door

Plant-room doors now take part in `nearestJoint`, including closed `onOpen` doors. The app retains one nearest closed-door neighbour alongside its existing pool-loop neighbours, gives an open plant door priority, and imports/builds the nearest room during idle time. It preserves the loop's existing neighbours when a plant door opens so opening and closing that door does not repeatedly rebuild them. Moving on replaces the prepared neighbour; stale pending imports do not mount rooms the walker no longer needs, and loading/preparing rooms are not disposed under an asynchronous preparation task.

Plant-room scene handles expose `prepare(width, height)`. While the root remains hidden, it sizes the camera and compositor, compiles scene materials asynchronously against the HDR target, then draws a compositor frame to prepare effects, textures and targets. It does not call the room's update loop or advance its controls, sound or simulation. The app waits for preparation before presenting the neighbour through its door, remembers completed preparation and clears readiness when a room is released. Preparation still has CPU/GPU work and can hitch on a first approach; this change moves it ahead of opening rather than making construction free.

Added a regression proving that closed plant doors compete by distance and automatic loop joints do not. In Chrome, the jacuzzi plant room was built, hidden and sized to 2565 × 1294 while its door remained shut. Opening the door then exposed the existing plant-room canvas with no runtime errors. The nearest plant room remained mounted after closing. No controlled frame-time comparison was made for this follow-up.

Validation: typecheck, lint (existing warnings only), formatting and production build passed. The full suite passed 1927 tests but hit timeouts in three long world/air-handling simulation tests while browser checks and other workspace work were running; those files and the doorway regression passed when rerun with one worker (23 passed, 1 skipped). Other concurrent changes in machine ports, room pipes and room services were left untouched.
