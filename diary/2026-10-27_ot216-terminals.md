## Problem Statement

Mission 091 replaces WH-CP1, the wet hall's chemical distribution panel, with two modern panels, one per chemical: Penfold OT-216 operator terminals with a 16 × 2 LCD, F1 MON, F2 SET, F3 CMD, ESC, ENTER and cursor keys, quantities in litres, linked to one control box that holds each day tank's levels, its inhibit and its fill-now. Partway through, the owner narrowed the scope: this is mostly a design task, so the panels go into the dev rooms only and nothing in the real game world changes yet (no world state, save or version bump, and WH-CP1 stays in the wet hall). The owner also asked for each stage to be committed and pushed as soon as it passed its checks.

## Our Approach

We kept the terminal free of plant logic, as the design asks: a pure state machine that is fed what the box sends (a `TerminalFeed`) and returns what it sends back (a `TerminalCommand`), with a test of every key in every state. The world's distribution model was out of bounds, so the box and its two lines became a trial model of their own beside the terminal logic. It reuses the world's day tank sizes and carries per-tank setpoints in litres, inhibit and fill-now, the box's isolator, MCB and a controller that can hang, AUTO following the box and HAND not needing it, and each terminal's link. The views bind the two: the terminal view reads the trial's feed and sends commands into it, and the box view's own isolator and MCB parts drive the trial. A trial station gives access to the local controls and the faults.
