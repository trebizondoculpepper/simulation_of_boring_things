# itch.io releases, sounds, and electric valves

The owner asked for the game to release to itch.io automatically. CI now has an `itch` job: after the checks pass on `main`, it pushes `dist/` with butler to `salimfadhley/the-pool`, channel `html5`, labelled with the build's version, logging in with the `BUTLER_API_KEY` repository secret. The first upload (0.1.237) was made by hand from a clean worktree while the workflow change waited for the `gh` login's `workflow` scope; CI's first run then pushed 0.1.238 as a patch. `doc/release_process.md` records the whole process, how to make and store the key without pasting it (read it from butler's `butler_creds` file), and the errors met on the way. The itch.io page's embed size, "played in the browser" and visibility are set by hand on its edit page; nothing in butler sets them. Vibe Top List was looked at: without a server or player accounts only a vote link and the public listing could be used, so it waits until the game has a listing.

Sounds. The jacuzzi cabinet's air blower used the squirrel-cage `blower` tone, much like the jet pump's `pump`; it now has its own `side-channel` tone, a regenerative blower's 2.3 kHz siren over a hiss. Dev7 had eleven places for thirteen tones; it now has places for all fourteen. The drain gurgle was resynthesised from a recording the owner chose: its spectrum and envelopes were measured (third-octave levels, each band's level, pulse rate and the glug resonance's track), the recipe rebuilt as four layers at those levels, and the render analysed the same way until each band was within about 3 dB up to 6 kHz. The method, download with yt-dlp, measure with numpy and scipy under `uv run`, render the loop through a throwaway vitest file to a WAV, compare, is worth reusing for other sounds.

Plant Room 1's eleven valves were pneumatic (an earlier reading of "solenoid valves"); the owner wanted servo drives. `createActuatedButterflyValve` takes `{ drive: 'electric' }`: a spring-return electric actuator with a gear-motor sound, a relay click and the end-stop thud, needing no air; the lounge valves' world model springs them shut on loss of power only. The Olympic room's stay pneumatic. Dev37, a valve test rig (source, DN80 pipe, one electric valve, flowmeter, a Penfold panel and a throw switch to cut the valve's power), was built by a sub-agent. Open: whether the rig's valve should stay shut after power returns until OPEN is pressed again, and the isolator's fixed "INCOMING SUPPLY ISOLATOR" title.

Also: the jacuzzi music is heard in its plant room 30 dB down; the fibre illuminator console moved off the music rack; the spa cabinets' doors have outside louvres matching their inside covers. A pull's conflict with uncommitted music work was resolved by renumbering its save to version 25.

Lesson: this project formats with `vp fmt`, not prettier; `npx prettier --write` rewrote five files' quotes before it was caught and undone.
