# Hall 5: Reg's photographs through the decades

Generated one original photograph for every scene in `doc/prompts/day-in-the-life/midjourney-prompts.md`: 30 horizontal 1536 × 1024 PNGs, ten decades from the 1920s to the 2020s, each with morning, midday and night. Used the built-in image generator, preserving the scene prompts and their photographic looks, adding a consistent fictional gallery framing and explicit no-logo/no-readable-text constraints. The three 1930s prompts had said “1920s sepia”; their date was corrected for generation and in the prompt. The first 2000s night image put Reg at the pool although the scene says his pager goes off at home; a second image explicitly placed him in his kitchen, and the inaccurate first was discarded. The chosen file for that scene is `2000s-night_b.png`; the rest are `_a.png`.

Viewed the images together as a contact sheet and inspected the 1920s opening, 1930s bleach scene, 1970s empty-pool vacuum and corrected 2000s pager scene at full size. The generation task is done. The images are candidates in `doc/gens/day-in-the-life/`; the Hall 5 prompt still calls for choosing, resizing and installing them, plus its walkthrough, benchmark and voice clips.
