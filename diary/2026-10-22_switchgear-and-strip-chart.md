# Switch room board and chart recorder

The unlabelled cabinet beside the switch room's other gear was the Voltara distribution board, `SR-DB3`. Its solid front panel obscured the modelled breakers and circuit chart, and the door had no use action. I rebuilt the carcass and door as an open frame with glazing, put the asset name on the door, and made the leaf open and close from a click. The same component is now beside the switch-fuse in dev0 as `DEV-DB1`, with a direct start view at `?room=dev0&view=switchgear`.

The strip-chart recorder's chart paper was 0.2 mm above the casing face, too little separation for stable distant depth testing. The paper, scale, pens, and glass now sit at distinct depths. The chart still draws from the same readings.

Typecheck, lint, formatting, tests, and build passed. The switch room and dev0 were checked in the browser; the board's labelled interior and opening door were visible.
