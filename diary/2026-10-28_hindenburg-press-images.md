# Development Diary Entry - 2026-10-28

## Context

The owner requested images for all eleven scenes in `doc/prompts/hinderburg/midjourney-prompts.md`, then requested three variants of each for selection.

## Work

Generated the images with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool and saved them as `doc/gens/hindenburg/<scene>_a.png`, `_b.png`, and `_c.png`. The nine press scenes use a fictional 1997 British regional-newspaper look; the two central-case scenes use neutral museum product photography. The prompts kept the location and people anonymous, with no intended readable brands or signs.

## Result

All 33 PNGs are present and have distinct file hashes. The 27 press images are 1536 × 1024; the six product photographs are 1448 × 1086. No image has yet been selected for the game or added to its runtime assets.
