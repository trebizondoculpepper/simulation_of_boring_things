# Pool of the Future photographs

Generated two further variants of each of Hall 5's three future scenes, giving the owner nine original images in `doc/gens/day-in-the-life/` (`_a`, `_b`, `_c` for each scene). Each set keeps the same augmented Reg across the impossible pool, the water-model inspection and the skimmer check.

Selected the matching `_b` set for the gallery and converted it to 900 × 600 JPEGs, with 225 × 150 low-resolution copies for the photo-detail system. Rewrote the captions beneath the prints to describe the displayed scenes as Reg's opening, testing and closing round. Kept the other variants as alternatives and marked the image prompt done.
