# Ladder controls, 2026-10-24

The owner asked for deliberate ladder mounting: Use at the bottom or top, forward to climb, back to descend, then ordinary movement after leaving the ladder. The old shared helper required looking up or down while walking, but no room supplied ladder zones to the controller, so the visible ladders did not use that logic. Replaced it with explicit mount state in the common first-person controller, added world-space zones for visible pool ladders, and connected PR_A, the pool halls, the donut pool and the arched corridor. The controller holds horizontal position on the rungs and exits onto the correct side at either end. Verified PR_A in Chrome by climbing from the pool and descending from the deck; both ends returned to walking. Half-Life's automatic contact and view-relative climbing informed the comparison, but this game uses Use to avoid accidental grabs at a pool edge.
