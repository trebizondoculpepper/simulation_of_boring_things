## Problem Statement

The owner asked for every bridge-style display to boot: black, 2–3 s of an emblem (a laurel-wreathed ringed disc like the starship service's seal, its stars replaced by stylised pool water, our own drawing), a 0.5 s fade to black, then the interface drawing in and becoming operational. Generic and reusable, shown in dev48 and dev49 with a BOOT ALL control, then used on the bridge's entrance alongside the lights coming up.

## Our Approach

Put the whole boot in the `ui2d` kit, which knows nothing of the game: a `BootScreen` node that wraps any tree, a pure `bootState(t)` for its timing, a `BootGroup` for a room of screens, and `drawEmblem` for the seal. The draw-in needed a way to paint a tree partly without touching every component, so `UiNode.paint` gained a veil hook (`paintVeiled`): while a veil is active, each leaf asks it whether to draw and under what clip or alpha. Elbows and bars sweep out; other leaves appear one at a time by place.

## Implementation

`src/ui2d/boot.ts`, `emblem.ts`, `boot.test.ts`; the veil in `node.ts`. `touch-surface.ts` wraps every screen in a `BootScreen` (on by default, so nothing changes until a room boots it) and keeps a `BootGroup` per kit (`bootGroupOf`), repainting at up to 15 Hz while booting. `views/boot-all-panel.ts` is the DISPLAY BOOT panel, the first exhibit in dev48 and dev49: built first, it has the screens built after it boot as they are made, so the room boots on entry. On the bridge, `createBridgeLightsUp().enter` now reports whether the entrance plays; if so `bootAll` with `ENTRANCE_BOOT`, otherwise `skipAll`. The entrance's glow moved to 3.0 s and the main lights' darkness to 4.0 s so the glow rises as the interfaces draw in and the main lights follow.

## Outcomes

- The emblem reads well at screen size: laurel of twelve leaf pairs, grey ring, navy disc with a caustic net, a red-and-white lane rope, ripples and twinkling glints, FEDERATED MUNICIPAL BATHS beneath.
- The first emblem draft clustered its caustic cells to one side (a poor scatter) and left a gap under the seal; a jittered grid and crossing stems fixed both.
- Screenshots are too slow to catch every phase in the game, so the draw-in was checked by painting a test tree at fixed progress onto a canvas in the page, and the bridge's entrance by dispatching the corridor hand-over event.
- The full test suite's two 5 s timeouts (world, Wash-O-Tron) passed when rerun alone.

## Next Steps

Other displays (character LCDs, CRTs) could take their own boots through the same timing. The emblem's legend is configurable per screen if the owner wants different authorities on different consoles.
