## Skimmer item-reveal music: original synthesis

The four cues in `resources/sound/music/item-reveal/` were made for this project on 2026-10-29 by direct additive synthesis from an original score, following `doc/prompts/music/item_reveal/item-reveal-prompts.md`. `resources/sound/music/item-reveal/generate.py` is the complete generator; the folder's README records the file timings and encoding. No music model, source recording, sample, existing melody or reference audio was used.

## The bridge's ambience: original synthesis

The bridge's room tone (`src/toolkit/components/sound/audio/bridge-ambience.ts`, `src/toolkit/components/sound/logic/bridge-ambience.ts`, mission 095) is the project's own work, synthesised live in Web Audio from oscillators, generated noise, filters and a seeded chirp scheduler. No recording is sampled, copied or downloaded: nothing from Star Trek or any upload taken from it. It was written in the spirit of the Next Generation bridge's background, from published descriptions of it. For character only, not sampled: the description of [control_room.wav](https://freesound.org/people/Diboz/sounds/211683/) by [Diboz](https://freesound.org/people/Diboz/) (CC0), "ambient sound akin to the bridge of a starship".

## The piezo beeps: synthesised

The piezo beep library (`resources/sound/effects/piezo/*.mp3`, mission 100) is the project's own work, every file synthesised from scratch on 2026-10-28 by `resources/sound/effects/piezo/generate.py` (a modelled piezo sounder, its housing and fascia, and synthesised room reverbs; method in that folder's `index.md` and `doc/research/piezo-beeper-sounds.md`). No recording, sample or copyrighted sound is used, analysed or imitated.

## The bridge's console sounds: owner-supplied, resynthesised

The bridge's console sounds (touch presses on its consoles, its displays booting, their background chatter, the unaccepted-alarm sounder at Central Control and its doors' card readers) are the owner's own console sounds, supplied on 2026-10-27 and resynthesised by Claude the same day with `resources/sound/effects/resynthesized_bloops/resynth.py` (sine partials tracked from each original's spectrogram, plus shaped noise; method and closeness in that folder's `index.md`). No sample of the originals is shipped: they stay in the git-ignored `resources/sound/effects/science_fiction_bleeps/`. Used so far (`src/toolkit/components/sound/logic/console-sounds.ts`): `ack_trill`, `actioning`, `amber_alert` (the alarm sounder), `coming_out_of_warp`, `correct_entry`, `engines_online`, `lock_out`, `look_here`, `now_online`, `processing`, `system_booting_up` and `we_are_being_hailed`. The other alarm-like files are kept in the folder for later.
