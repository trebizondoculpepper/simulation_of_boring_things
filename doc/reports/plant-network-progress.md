## Step 1: the pools' water circuits, done

`src/game/plant/network/`, pure and tested (`network.test.ts`, 23 tests):

- **The network** (`types.ts`, `pool-water.ts`, `plant-network.ts`): 117 nodes and 178 pipes. Every machine and every valve is a node with two identities (the owner's rule): a serial number, the order it was added (`#88` for that valve), permanent and kept in an append-only register (`serials.ts`, `serial-register.ts`, new entries from `ASSIGN_SERIALS=1 npx vp test run scripts/assign-serials.test.ts`), and a proximal ID saying what and where (`pl2.f3.bv2`), which is its plate's tag in lowercase (`PL2-F3-BV2`). The lounge and Olympic circuits run balance tank → duty and standby pump sets (suction valve, strainer, pump, non-return valve, discharge valve) → filters, each with its six valves (inlet, outlet, backwash supply and waste, rinse, drain) → ozone contact tank → heat exchanger → UV → hypochlorite and acid injection → pool, which returns over its overflow and through its main drain. The balance tank has its float valve and drain, the pool its drain-down valve. The jacuzzi and footbath are each a packaged set. The systems still to build are placeholder boundaries: make-up, drainage, the LTHW ring, the chemical stores, the electrical supply.
- **Its rules, checked** (`validate.ts`): unique, well-formed IDs and tags; every pipe joins declared ports of its own service; every port is joined; every circuit runs from tank to pool and back; every drain reaches the drainage.
- **Its water, solved** (`water.ts`, `state.ts`) on the existing solver (`src/game/sim/hydraulics.ts`), each circuit on its own, in milliseconds. Dead ends and shut sections are pruned before solving (Newton's method oscillates on them) and take their neighbour's head. Valves close on an equal-percentage characteristic, so the last turns do most of the throttling. On the model, now: each pool gets its design flow from its duty pump, within 1 %; changeover to the standby pump; a shut suction valve stops the circuit; a throttled discharge valve cuts the flow; an isolated filter's share moves to the others; a backwashed filter runs up through its bed to waste while the rest serve the pool; the balance tank drains; the pool drains down; one pool's plant leaves another's alone.
- **The gaps** (`gaps.ts`, `doc/reports/model-gaps.md`, regenerated by `MODEL_GAPS=1 npx vp test run scripts/model-gaps.test.ts`): 59 placeholders, largest first: water chemistry (19), electrics (11), chemical supply (6), levels not stepped (6), ozone (4), heating (3), float valves, UV dose, packaged-set internals, the overflow weir (2 each), make-up and drainage (1 each). A test pins the count so it only shrinks.

## Step 2: make-up and drainage, done

`src/game/plant/network/wet-hall.ts`, with `common.ts` now holding what every part shares (the builder, the placeholders, the site's boundaries, the heights):

- **Heights.** The rooms are separate scenes, not one building, so the network keeps its own heights, from the plant sizing's levels: metres above the wet hall's floor, 4 m a storey, each pool's deck 3.5 m above its plant room's floor (the lounge and footbath on level 0, the Olympic on 1, the jacuzzi on 2), the backwash holding tank sunk in its pit at −1 m, the sewer reached at +1 m.
- **Make-up.** The town main (a real boundary, at 30 m) through its stop valve and the break tank's float valve over its Category 5 air gap; the break tank's outlet valve; the duty and standby booster pumps (each with suction, non-return and discharge valves); the make-up meter; the make-up main valve; the make-up main; then each plant room's own branch isolating valve (`pl1.mu.iv1` … `pl4.mu.iv1`) to its balance tank's float valve or its packaged set.
- **Drainage.** Each plant room's drainage header takes its filters' backwash, rinse and drain valves, its tank's drain and its pool's drain-down, and falls to the wet hall's drainage main and the backwash holding tank. The tank's discharge pump lifts it through its rising main to the foul sewer (a real boundary) at the consented 10 m³/h; the tank's drain valve lets the pump take it below its normal suction.
- **Placeholders retired.** The make-up and drainage placeholder boundaries are gone, their serials (1 and 2) retired in the register.
- **On the model, now:** a balance tank tops up through the booster set and the meter; the jacuzzi's plant, on the top level, has pressure to spare; with the booster stopped, or a room's branch valve shut, nothing tops up; the standby booster takes over; the break tank refills from the town main, and not with its stop valve shut; a backwash runs into the holding tank; the holding tank goes to the sewer at about the consented rate, only with its pump running.
- **The solver, fixed** (`src/game/sim/hydraulics.ts`): its Jacobian was estimated with a step of a millionth of each head, which on a short joint high above the datum spoiled the estimate, so the make-up chain stalled short of balance (and the Olympic circuit did once raised to its level). It now steps a fixed tenth of a micrometre; a regression test rebuilt from the stalled chain fails on the old solver and passes on the new. The circulation and every other test using it still pass.
- **The gaps:** 65 (152 nodes, 221 pipes): the wet hall adds its tanks' levels, overflows, float valve and three pumps' electrics, and the two placeholder boundaries go. The pin moved from 59 to 65 with this step, as new plant brings its own placeholders.

## Noted for step 6 (the rooms drawn from the model)

The model has more valves than the rooms draw: in the Olympic plant room it has 40 (each pump set's suction, non-return and discharge valves; each filter's six; the tank's float and drain valves; the main drain and drain-down valves), where the room draws 21 (four per filter and the tank drain). The drawn filter "drain" valve is tagged `DV1`, which the naming rules reserve for a pump's discharge valve; the model uses `WV1`. Both will be reconciled when the rooms are drawn from the network.
