The plan fits Hall 4’s pure-logic structure, but I would fix these points before implementing it:

- **CT9’s maximum is wrong for this model.** The plan gives 7 kg of sodium thiosulphate to remove 10 mg/l chlorine from 500 m³. The model is calibrated to **5 g per m³ for each 1 mg/l removed**, so that change needs about **25 kg** ([pool-chemistry.ts](/Users/salimfadhley/workspace/simulation_of_boring_things/src/game/water/pool-chemistry.ts:169), [pool-chemistry.ts](/Users/salimfadhley/workspace/simulation_of_boring_things/src/game/water/pool-chemistry.ts:616)). [PWTAG TN23](https://www.pwtag.org/superchlorination-of-swimming-pool-water-february-2014/) gives the same working figure and advises slight underdosing. Correct the maximum and its rationale together.

- **The formatting rules conflict.** “Two significant figures” and “one decimal place under 10” cannot both hold for Tiny doses such as CT9’s proposed 0.07 kg. Rounding that to 0.1 kg also destroys the advertised √10 progression. Specify precision for values below 1, from 1–10, and from 10–100 separately; test displayed amounts and ratios separately from the unrounded amounts passed to the model.

- **“Largest sensible single dose” needs a clearer basis.** CT5’s 25 kg comes from a _daily_ supplier rate, which does not establish a single dose or a 0.4 pH change. CT1’s 40 l adds about 11.2 mg/l chlorine using the model’s 140 g/l strength, rather than the stated 10 ([museum-4-lab.ts](/Users/salimfadhley/workspace/simulation_of_boring_things/src/game/plant/museum-4-lab.ts:75)). State whether each maximum is a practical ceiling or a rounded target dose, and check its actual effect in the chemistry model.

- **Avoid duplicating tank facts.** IDs, units, product names, strengths, purposes and side effects already live in `TANKS` ([museum-4-lab.ts](/Users/salimfadhley/workspace/simulation_of_boring_things/src/game/plant/museum-4-lab.ts:39)). Key the new table by `TankId`, derive units and names from the tank where possible, and make coverage checks catch both missing and extra IDs. A test that two duplicated unit fields agree only detects drift after it occurs.

- **Sources and tests need more substance.** “PWTAG,” “HSG179,” and “suppliers’ charts” are too broad to audit individual maxima. Give each entry a specific document, section or URL, product strength, and calculation. Add model-level checks for representative doses, especially acid, CO₂, fresh-water dilution and thiosulphate; the proposed tests otherwise verify table consistency while missing incorrect chemistry.
