# The Pool

The poolrooms are serene, whimsical, impossible spaces. Sunlight falls across still water. Tiled corridors lead somewhere they shouldn't. Everything is strangely, beautifully clean.

Somebody has to keep it that way.

Behind the tiles lies another reality: the plant rooms. No less labyrinthine, but governed by engineering, logic and cause and effect. Every pipe goes somewhere. Every valve has a purpose. These two worlds depend on each other: the impossible pools and the entirely necessary machinery that keeps them alive.

**The Pool** takes you into the real backrooms behind the most liminal of spaces. If the poolrooms existed, what would it take to keep all that water warm, clear and safe to swim in?

Enter a world of real-time swimming pool maintenance. Balance tanks, circulation pumps, sand-filter backwashing, chemical dosing, automated valves and control panels. Oh, so many control panels. Turn their switches, watch their gauges and trace their pipes. The machinery has a job to do, and your interventions have consequences.

Experience the quiet satisfaction of a plant running properly. Discover how an empty sodium hypochlorite tank can turn pristine water into a problem. Follow a fault from its first warning lamp through the interconnected systems behind it.

Then step back into the poolrooms. Listen to the water. Admire the light.

It takes a remarkable amount of machinery to make somewhere feel this peaceful.
