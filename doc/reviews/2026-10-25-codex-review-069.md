# Review of 069: machine states

The rule that a state needs evidence is sound, but the proposed table crosses three different boundaries: persisted `World` state, values derived from that state, and private scene or panel simulations. The first two can support a world-backed overlay; the third cannot until it is moved into or bound to the world. In particular, [world.ts](../../src/game/world.ts) contains no ozone-generator or boiler-controller state, and [component-descriptions.ts](../../src/game/plant/component-descriptions.ts) currently has no status binding for either generator or boiler. The following checks concern what the model can establish, not merely what a panel can display.

| Proposed kind | What the world can establish; corrections to the vocabulary |
|---|---|
| Pump (motor starter) | [motor-starters.ts](../../src/game/plant/motor-starters.ts) stores `mode`, AUTO `command`, `running`, a latched `tripped`, and a starvation timer; [world.ts](../../src/game/world.ts) supplies room power, local isolator, pool low-low hold and the separate panel emergency stop. Thus tripped, stopped by emergency stop, unpowered, isolated, selector OFF, running in HAND, running in AUTO, and AUTO stopped by explicit STOP or by duty/standby demand are distinguishable. Add a **held off by low-low balance tank** state: `pools[pool].tripped` is this hold, not the motor's latched trip, and it clears on recovery. Do not label every motor trip “starved / low-low tank”: low-low never latches the starter, and `starved` is a seconds counter reset on stopping, so after a trip it does not prove its cause. The only implemented trip mechanism is starvation, but the saved starter records no trip reason. Add **running without flow / starving** while `running` and `starved > 0`, if that live distinction is desired. `running` is the actual state; HAND, AUTO and commands alone do not prove motion. An AUTO pump with `command: false` is explicitly STOPped; `command: null` can mean the standby is not wanted, so those are provably different causes within the last proposed “Stopped” state. |
| Pump set | [motor-starters.ts](../../src/game/plant/motor-starters.ts) can enumerate the two starters duty first and report each independently. It has no separate pump-set controller state or set-level trip. “Each pump's state” is a defensible composite, provided the added low-low hold and the limits on trip cause carry through; if a single set state is wanted, that needs its own rule. |
| Air handling unit | [air-handling.ts](../../src/game/plant/air-handling.ts) persists local isolator, selector, hall occupancy, damper travel, fan speed, heat-pump state, filter pressure and trip; [pool-ahu.ts](../../src/toolkit/components/plant/logic/pool-ahu.ts) shows the sequence. A trip can be identified as **blocked filters**, and room supply, local isolation, OFF and unoccupied AUTO can be identified. “Running, heat pump on/off” is proved only from actual speed (`> 0.05` is the controller's run-lamp threshold) and `heatPump`; HAND alone does not prove running. The table misses **dampers opening / starting** and **stopping / running down** while speed and damper position change. “Idle (AUTO, hall unoccupied)” does not cover every stopped AUTO case during transitions. A latched trip can coexist briefly with nonzero speed, so precedence must decide what that combined condition says. |
| Boiler cascade | [boiler-gas.ts](../../src/game/plant/boiler-gas.ts) proves ECV position, emergency-stop position, methane detection and latched SSOV position; [services.ts](../../src/game/plant/services.ts) proves boiler-house electrical supply and heat-source availability. **SSOV shut** does not by itself prove “gas safety tripped”: ECV shut is a separate cause, stop pressed or gas detected are present causes, and an SSOV that stays shut after they clear is a reset-required latch. “No gas (ECV shut, emergency stop)” merges distinct conditions, and the stop acts through the SSOV. The world cannot prove **Firing** or **Standing by (no heat demand)** from delivered pool heat or `plant.sources.heat`: heating is an aggregate service calculation, without a boiler firing rate, burner status or heat-demand field in `World`. [boiler-house.ts](../../src/toolkit/components/plant/logic/boiler-house.ts) has individual `BoilerState` values for OFF, purge, ignition, burner on, pump run-on, blocked and lockout, but these are outside `World`; the proposed cascade row should not claim them as world states today. |
| Booster set | [wet-hall.ts](../../src/game/plant/wet-hall.ts) proves a latched low-break-tank trip and wet-hall supply. `boosterRunning` means **make-up water was delivered in the last step** (`flows.drawn > 0`), and `flow` gives its rate; it is not independent motor feedback. “Running” is defensible only with that meaning, and “Standing by” only means no delivery, not that a healthy motor has been verified. The row misses the separate **low break-tank level before/after a trip** and can distinguish no demand from no supply only to the extent the world's make-up call is inspected. A trip remains latched after the tank refills. |
| Jacuzzi cabinet | [jacuzzi-cabinet.ts](../../src/game/plant/jacuzzi-cabinet.ts) stores POWER and a latched `no-flow` trip; [world.ts](../../src/game/world.ts) derives room-plus-isolator supply, valve path, external tank low-low, pump call and circulation. The row's no-power, switch OFF, no-flow trip, running without flow and circulating states are provable, but **tank low-low hold** is missing: it stops the pump and recovers automatically, whereas `cabinet.trip` is a no-flow latch. Distinguish room supply lost from local isolator open if the cause wording is intended to be exact. The current `component-status.ts` collapses `cabinet.trip` and `pools.pool_03.tripped` into “Tripped”; that existing wording does not make them the same fault. |
| UV unit | [uv.ts](../../src/game/plant/uv.ts) proves only whether the lamp is lit, from circulation and its named local isolator. The world also has the plant-room supply, so **supply unavailable**, **local isolator open**, and **no circulation** are individually detectable reasons for Off. It has no UV lamp-failure or trip state; “Unpowered” is justified only for loss of room supply or an open isolator, not for every `uvLampOn === false`. The table misses **Off because circulation is absent**. |
| Ozone generator | The [generator block](../../src/game/plant/ozone.ts) itself defines, in precedence order, **off** (no power), **tripped: _cause_** (latched cooling-water, dryer, vacuum, door-open or ozone-in-room fault), **blocked: no flow** (circulation below minimum; automatic interlock, not latched), **running**, and **standby** (powered but not enabled). It also exposes `running`, `tripped`, fault code, ozone output and power/ozone/alarm lamps. These are genuine generator-model states, but the installed [ozone room](../../src/toolkit/components/plant/logic/ozone-room.ts) keeps its `System`, MAINS, START/STOP, HAND/OFF/AUTO, faults and reset privately, and [room-equipment.ts](../../src/toolkit/gallery/views/room-equipment.ts) creates that room per scene with a private `wanted` flag. `World` holds only the room supply, named isolator and independent room-gas detector; it cannot prove whether this installed generator is running, blocked, tripped, enabled or producing ozone. The ozone row can be filled with the block vocabulary above **only if its state source is explicitly the generator block**; as a world-backed row today it can say at most that external electrical supply is available or absent. `pools.ts` uses the ozone isolator for chloramine treatment but does not bind that chemistry to this generator's private trip or output. |
| Gas detector | [gas.ts](../../src/game/plant/gas.ts) stores each zone's detector `reading`, latched `a1` and `a2`, and acceptance via [gas-detection.ts](../../src/toolkit/components/general-safety/logic/gas-detection.ts). A2 alarm before A1 warning before a numeric reading is supported, but **A2 accepted/sounder silenced** is another provable distinction, as is an alarm still latched after the reading falls. Do not infer “safe” from a low current reading. There is no detector power or detector-failure state to justify Unpowered or Faulty. Also, chlorine detectors have a 0–5 ppm range while ozone detectors have 0–1 ppm; the current formatter's comment that all gas readings have a 0–1 ppm range is inaccurate. |
| Lounge solenoid valve | [lounge-valves.ts](../../src/game/plant/lounge-valves.ts) derives actual position `0..1` from command and room power, with spring return when power is lost. Open and shut are provable at endpoints; opening and shutting require comparing position with the **powered target**, not command alone. **Spring returning toward shut** is provable while supply is lost and position remains above zero; once fully shut, the world proves “shut, power absent” but not a distinct spring-motion state. The world can also prove **commanded open but unable to open because power is absent**. Current [component-status.ts](../../src/game/plant/component-status.ts) reports the installed command as valve position, which is wrong for these travelling valves. |
| Manual valve | [installed-valves.ts](../../src/game/plant/installed-valves.ts) stores Boolean positions for installed valves, and identified model parts may store an `open` Boolean, so Open/Shut is supported for genuinely manual valves. Do not apply this row by `valve` kind alone: lounge actuated valves share `installedValves` as commands and need the travel record for actual position. |

## Precedence and the owner's questions

The precedence proposal needs an explicit distinction between **current physical behavior**, **inhibiting cause**, and **latched fault**. A first-match list loses information when several truths coexist: a pump may be tripped, isolated and in OFF; an AHU may be tripped while its fan coasts down; an actuated valve may be commanded open while spring-returning after power loss. The example “Tripped” before “Unpowered” means a dark, isolated pump still reads Tripped; “Unpowered” before “Tripped” hides a reset-required fault. Both are defensible display choices, so the table should document what the chosen single status optimizes for and preserve the other facts for inspection. Cause-specific text should follow the actual model condition and not retrospectively infer a trip cause from a timer that has reset.

On question 1, the code establishes that **isolated** and **selector OFF** can both be true and offers no player-priority rule to choose between them. The same issue applies to trip versus loss of power and to transient motion versus an inhibit. This is a wording and precedence decision for the owner, not a fact settled by the model.

On question 2, controller state in [boiler-house.ts](../../src/toolkit/components/plant/logic/boiler-house.ts) is detailed enough to support “switched off at its controller,” burner stages and lockouts when it is made shared world state; [boiler-gas.ts](../../src/game/plant/boiler-gas.ts) presently shares only the gas safety chain. Moving controller state into `World` would make the proposed richer boiler row possible and require save-shape/version handling. Whether that scope is wanted belongs to the owner.

On question 3, the distinction is real in [motor-starters.ts](../../src/game/plant/motor-starters.ts): `mode: 'off'` is a local selector position, whereas `mode: 'auto'` with `command: false` is a STOP command, and an AUTO standby with `command: null` can be unwarranted because duty is running. Keeping “Off” and “Stopped” separate would expose those different control conditions; whether the overlay needs that granularity is the owner's choice.
