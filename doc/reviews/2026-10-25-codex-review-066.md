## Answers for the reviewer

- **Shared chaser: yes, as an opt-in addition.** [MimicSpec](../../src/toolkit/components/industrial-control/logic/mimic.ts) already describes each printed line separately, and [createMimicBoard](../../src/toolkit/components/industrial-control/views/mimic-board.ts) draws those lines once onto the board texture before making live lamps. Adding live LEDs only for lines with a flow source can leave every existing board's lines and appearance unchanged. Keep the original printed line under the LEDs and leave lines without a source on the present rendering path. The current `show(state)` advances an internal clock by a fixed 1/60 per call, so a 0.12 s *real-time* chaser needs elapsed time or a monotonic clock rather than counting calls; the local panel and control-room update paths must both drive it. Split a pipe at tees and valves where its segments can carry different flows, and mark the LED objects live so room batching does not freeze them.

- **True backwash: the four filter valves can make the stated path; no additional filter valve is missing for it.** [installed-valves.ts](../../src/game/plant/installed-valves.ts) supplies each filter's inlet IV1, outlet IV2, backwash-to-waste BV1 and drain DV1. In [room-pipes.ts](../../src/toolkit/gallery/logic/room-pipes.ts), IV1 sits above the inlet/backwash tee, BV1 below that tee to waste, and IV2 lies between the filter outlet and the common filtered-water header. With the other filter serving, shut the washed filter's IV1 and DV1, open its IV2 and BV1: water can leave the other filter's outlet, traverse the outlet header and washed filter's outlet in reverse, rise through its bed, then leave via BV1. [lounge-schematic.ts](../../src/game/plant/lounge-schematic.ts) contains those links. This is a path through the *filtered outlet header*, before the heat exchanger, contact tank and UV, so the board should not label its source as the downstream pool return. The present [loungeValveFlows](../../src/game/plant/lounge-valves.ts) does not calculate that path: it adds waste from `inlet * BV1`, gives no signed per-filter flow, treats pump discharge valves as a single maximum for pool flow, and can report waste even when no discharge valve is open. Those equations need a coherent replacement, including partially travelling valves and the case where the other filter is unavailable. There is no separate backwash-in valve on the lounge battery; IV2 performs that function in this particular route. The Olympic five-valve layout is different and should not silently be copied here.

- **Control-room space: no complete fit under the current room and packing rules.** [shell-rooms.ts](../../src/toolkit/gallery/logic/shell-rooms.ts) makes `control_room` 5.5 × 8.5 m; [room-equipment.ts](../../src/toolkit/gallery/logic/room-equipment.ts) packs three mimics, the west-wall annunciator, other instruments and the staff noticeboard, with a 0.3 m wall-panel gap and door/feeder clearances. I ran its `packRoom` with a 2.5 × 1.3 m framed lounge board (the 2.4 × 1.2 m spec plus the size allowance used by [control.ts](../../src/toolkit/gallery/rooms/plant/control.ts)). Inserted before the annunciator with north/west/east wall choices, the new board landed on the east wall but `sign:staff-noticeboard` failed to place. Trying other insertion positions and wall preferences, including south, did not place every item. The prompt's instruction to report a failed fit therefore applies. The owner needs to decide which existing item moves or whether the room changes before this board is installed; do not silently shrink the board or drop the noticeboard. This was a packer probe, not a visual clearance check.

- **Board wording for the owner:** Show the real pump identifiers **P-201A** and **P-201B**, or establish clearly that P1/P2 are shorthand for them; the current local mimic prints P1/P2 while the starters and door indicators use those motor tags. Put **BACKWASH TO WASTE** at each BV1 branch and **FILTERED OUTLET HEADER / BACKWASH SUPPLY** at the common source, avoiding the ambiguous "return header" for water that has not yet passed the downstream treatment. State the sign convention beside the two readings, for example **+ SERVICE / − BACKWASH**, and label them **F1 FLOW** and **F2 FLOW** with m³/h. The current ten-column LCD legend formatter in [mimic.ts](../../src/toolkit/components/industrial-control/logic/mimic.ts) would shorten `F1 FLOW m³/h` to `F1 FL m³/h`; use a shorter legend or provide a separate printed name. Let the owner inspect the final board artwork and labels at the intended viewing distance before shipping.

## Gaps and risks in the plan

- The new meter cannot be made model-backed by registering it in [installed-fixtures.ts](../../src/game/plant/installed-fixtures.ts) and adding it to a `PipeRun` alone. [room-pipework.ts](../../src/toolkit/gallery/views/room-pipework.ts) binds a meter to the enhanced network only when its run supplies `m.fitting`; [lounge-schematic.ts](../../src/game/plant/lounge-schematic.ts) currently has no flowmeter fittings. Add PL1-F1-FT1 and PL1-F2-FT1 to the lounge network as fittings on the outlet links, then draw each from that fitting and read the same signed world calculation. Also update `room-pipework.ts`'s `readings()`, which currently returns values only in `plant_pool_02`; otherwise new lounge meters will display zero.

- Define how the 34 m³/h backwash draw affects the other filter, total pump delivery and pool return. With the current fixed 45 m³/h per serving filter, one filter in service and 34 m³/h diverted through the other gives only 11 m³/h to the pool, not 56 m³/h. If 56 is intended, the serving filter or pump must carry more flow than the current model assigns. Test conservation (`pump delivery = pool return + waste`, apart from explicitly modelled drains), the pump-off state and both filters' valve combinations. The existing starter no-flow decision in [world.ts](../../src/game/world.ts) depends on `toPool + toWaste`, so incorrect waste flow can also prevent a legitimate no-flow trip.

- The proposed `MimicState` directions alone cannot express the specified darkness below 1 m³/h or flow during valve travel unless `loungeFilterMimicState` applies that threshold from numeric segment flows. Specify each segment's signed flow and which way its drawn points count as positive. Backwash reverses the washed outlet segment, but water still runs forward through the serving filter's outlet and waste runs toward the drain; a single state for the entire shared header would be misleading.

- The proposed pure layout test must account for the physical bounds of symbols, 19 cm readout housings, valve lamps, printed labels, 22 mm pushbuttons, the bottom mode/LAMP TEST row and the Penfold mark. [checkMimic](../../src/toolkit/components/industrial-control/logic/mimic.ts) currently checks only centre points, duplicate IDs and readout coverage, so it cannot prove the required 30 mm button clearance or absence of overlap. A legibility check in the rendered room remains necessary.

- Preserve the distinction between actual running and a START command. [motor-starters.ts](../../src/game/plant/motor-starters.ts) already ignores START/STOP outside AUTO, and a stopped or tripped motor has different causes; the new board should keep its RUN lamp bound to `running` and show OFF, HAND or trip status clearly enough that an apparently inert remote START is understandable. Both installations should send only the same world actions; selectors, trip RESET and the local emergency stop stay on the cubicle doors.
