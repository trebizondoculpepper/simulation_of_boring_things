# Music prompts: the skimmer's item reveal

Prompts for the short music cues of Hall 5's skimmer game (the owner, 2026-10-29), a parody of the treasure-chest moment in a 1990s 3D adventure game: reaching into the skimmer, the walker looks up as a slow, mysterious phrase builds suspense (what will it be?), then the find appears, turning in the air above, with a bright fanfare, and a banner says "You got a dead rat!". The joke is the gap between the music's grandeur and the thing found: hair, leaves, a dead rat, a dildo, a butt plug, more hair.

Every cue must be original. Ask for the feel of that era's treasure jingle, never its tune: no melody, motif or chord sequence quoted or paraphrased from any existing game, film or song. Each prompt below is complete, ready to paste into a music generator; generate a few takes of each and keep the best.

**Where the results go:** `resources/sound/music/item-reveal/<cue>.mp3` (or `.ogg`), named as each heading says. Until a file is supplied, the game plays a synthesised stand-in of the same shape. Note each kept take's source and generator in `CREDITS.md`.

Rules for every cue: instrumental only, no vocals, no lyrics, no speech; starts on its first note with no silence or fade-in; mono-compatible; mastered quiet enough to sit under the game's sounds (peaks around −6 dBFS); the length given is a target to within a quarter of a second, because the game times the walker's look-up and the item's appearance to it.

## 1. The suspense (`reveal-suspense`)

Plays as the walker reaches into the skimmer and looks up: originally about 2.5 seconds, extended to 3.05 seconds on 2026-10-29 to include more of the rising arpeggio, ending unresolved so the fanfare can land on the next beat.

```
Original short video-game music cue, 2.5 seconds, instrumental, no vocals. A mysterious, slowly rising phrase that builds suspense as a treasure chest creaks open: a soft shimmering string tremolo underneath, a harp or celesta climbing upward one note at a time in an eerie, unresolved minor-to-whole-tone pattern, a faint glassy synth pad swelling, getting slightly louder and brighter as it rises. Late-1990s console adventure game sound, warm sampled orchestra, slightly lo-fi like a cartridge soundtrack. Ends hanging on an unresolved suspended note, as if holding its breath, with no final chord and no fade-out. Tempo about 90 bpm, key centred on D. Entirely original melody, not based on any existing game, film or song.
```

## 2. The fanfare (`reveal-fanfare`)

Plays the instant the find appears above the walker: about 2.5 seconds, triumphant and sincere, as if the dead rat were a legendary sword.

```
Original short video-game victory fanfare, 2.5 seconds, instrumental, no vocals. The moment a hero holds a newly found treasure above their head: a quick bright pickup of three short brass notes rising into a big held major chord, trumpets and French horns over timpani and a cymbal swell, a glittering harp or glockenspiel sparkle on top, then the chord rings out and stops cleanly. Earnest, heroic, a little over the top. Late-1990s console adventure game sound, warm sampled orchestra, slightly lo-fi like a cartridge soundtrack. Key of G major, resolving the suspense of a cue in D, tempo about 120 bpm. Entirely original melody, not based on any existing game, film or song.
```

For the last find, more hair: the same fanfare's start, which loses heart. About 2.5 seconds.

Comic disappointment, deadpan, not slapstick. Late-1990s console adventure game sound, warm sampled orchestra, slightly lo-fi like a cartridge soundtrack. Key of G major, tempo about 120 bpm. Entirely original melody, not based on any existing game, film or song.
