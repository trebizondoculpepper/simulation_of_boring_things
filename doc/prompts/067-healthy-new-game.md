# 067: Define and maintain a healthy New Game

Status: researched and planned 2026-10-25; implementation pending. This is a repeatable maintenance mission. Its first execution establishes an executable healthy-start contract; later executions audit and repair drift as the game model evolves.

## The owner's request

A normal New Game should place the player in a normal, healthy pool with all facilities operational. Model changes may change which settings are normal. Work out the healthy state, record the reasoning and evidence, and make the work possible to repeat.

The maintained definition is [`doc/healthy-start.md`](../healthy-start.md). The [initial audit](../reports/healthy-start/2026-10-25-initial-audit.md) supplies the first measured baseline. This prompt plans future implementation; the research has not changed runtime defaults.

## Scope

Cover the whole current leisure centre, not just a pool's temperature and displayed flow: its circulation and treatment circuits, tanks, water and chemical services, starters, control modes, valves, heating, ventilation, gas and electrical safety, lighting and usable attractions. Enumerate these from the world and installed model/catalogue so new equipment cannot silently escape the audit. Classify every facility as running, ready/automatic, intentionally idle, faulty or not yet represented by a testable model, with a reason for the latter cases.

Treat steady state as a healthy operating envelope over time. Respect duty/standby operation, thermostat and float hysteresis, timed facilities, resource consumption and filter loading. Do not enable mutually exclusive machines or open every valve to interpret “all facilities operational”. Do not disable failures, auto-clear trips, refill stocks on every step or weaken alarm thresholds to manufacture a healthy result.

## Work packages, in order

1. **Re-establish the baseline.** Read this prompt, the maintained definition, previous reports and changes since the last run. Follow New Game from `createGameSession.start()` through `WorldStore.reset()` to `newWorld()`, then its first simulation steps. Compare the title's separately settled “normal” scenario. Rerun the observational script and record the source commit, dirty files, world version, start clock, ambient/load conditions, deterministic bather seed, integration step and audit coverage. On later runs, list added or changed model nodes, controls, roles, thresholds and migrations.
2. **Define healthy operation per subsystem.** Map configured targets, limits, normal valve roles, automatic controls, required supplies and actual output paths to the maintained contract. Establish bands, units and a handover period; distinguish user-facing faults from informational activity. Trace real quantities to the model and prove that circulation, reserve volume, heating capacity and chemical treatment are delivered. Identify display/model disagreements and room-local equipment not represented by the world. Resolve the playable start's time/session consistently; the current midnight start is a documented decision to revisit.
3. **Make the contract executable.** Add pure health checks returning structured findings with asset/path, measured value, expected range/condition, units, elapsed time, reason and severity. Keep health evaluation independent of the factory it checks; deriving a target from configuration does not excuse failing to check its service path. Have a coverage check that reports newly installed or changed equipment without a health rule or an explicit justified classification. Extend `src/game/new-game.test.ts` with meaningful first-frame and trajectory checks, including transient faults and required service availability. Add representative negative controls (closed circulation path, missing utility, low tank, gas alarm) to prove the checker detects faults, rather than merely validating its own fixture.
4. **Repair the playable preset.** Correct the source model, sizing, roles, control defaults or initial state when the evidence requires it. Prefer a deterministic model-derived playable initialiser, shared deliberately by New Game and the title's normal preset, without coupling their mutable worlds. Retain a low-level test constructor only if its purpose and callers are explicit. If settling is necessary, document a bounded procedure, a convergence/health test, transient history and startup cost; an arbitrary warm-up that hides faults is insufficient. Do not copy a stale serialized World or discard changed controls on load. Bump `WORLD_VERSION` and add a migration only if the saved shape changes, following project rules.
5. **Exercise normal control behavior and persistence.** Prove duty/standby readiness, make-up hysteresis, heating/ventilation control, and timed attraction start/stop from the healthy preset, using independent copies so probes do not alter the handover world. Verify fresh New Game replaces an unhealthy old save; Continue retains player changes; save/reload preserves the playable baseline; room changes and title shots do not reset, damage or advance the player's world. Check a new browser session and saved preferences separately from plant health.
6. **Soak, inspect and publish the evidence.** Check first steps, 60 s, 600 s and 3600 s at a suitable fine step with intermediate observations; then audit opening, busy/occupied operation, closing and a day. Compare production stepping and coarse catch-up where it affects controls. A resource or filter trend is not itself a defect: report when maintenance becomes necessary. Verify local panels, control-room annunciators, tank levels, flows, dosing and visible facilities in the browser, using a development room first for any new display/control. Record unsupported systems honestly. Update the maintained definition, a new dated report, this run history, TODO, changelog and diary; run project checks before committing.

## Acceptance

- A fresh playable game has the current centre's required services healthy and usable on its first frame, without player repairs, and remains within justified operating bands for the handover hour.
- No transient fault is hidden by looking only at later checkpoints. Duty/standby and automatic cycling work as intended. Normal informational activity is distinguishable from a fault.
- Actual calculated flows, volumes, temperatures, chemical treatment and interlocks support the displays; the audit does not pass merely because optimistic constructor values were copied into its expectations.
- Every currently installed subsystem has a health rule or an explicit documented coverage limitation. A new subsystem or changed model role causes the coverage report to change, rather than silently remaining “all healthy”.
- New Game, Continue, save/reload, room transitions and independent title scenarios behave correctly. The player's world is not altered by audit probes or attract preparation.
- There is one maintained definition, executable checks, a repeatable audit command and a dated evidence report with differences from the previous run. Remaining defects and follow-ups are in the root TODO.
