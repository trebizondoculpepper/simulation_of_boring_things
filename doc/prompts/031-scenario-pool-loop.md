# Prompt: scenario 8, a small pool loop end to end

Written by Claude (Opus 5.5, Claude Code), 2026-10-23, one of eight test scenarios the owner asked for (each proving a piece of modelling the Olympic pool's redo will need). Read `AGENTS.md`, then the scenarios already built: Dev21 (sources to sinks, `source-sink-plumbing.ts`), Dev22 (battery-backed pump, `battery-pump-rig.ts`), Dev23 (gravity drain, `jacuzzi-drain.ts`).

**Status:** Not started.

## The scenario

An IBC as the balance tank, a pump, a sand filter, the heat exchanger and back to the tank: a whole circulation, laid out by the room's layers (sizer, regions, placer, plumber, scorer) with no hand placement, then run.

## What it proves

- The whole pipeline on a real circuit, every machine from the scenarios before working together: the dress rehearsal for redoing the Olympic pool.

## Done when

- The scorer passes it on its gates; the circulation runs, heats and filters; each machine's own displays agree with the loop's model.
- A dev room shows it, and a report compares its score with Dev19's.
