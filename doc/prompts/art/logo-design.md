# Logo design prompts: The Pool

The owner's brief (2026-10-29): a logo for the game that carries its title, THE POOL, and shows its idea: above the surface, the sleek, modern, simple swimming pool; below it, vastly complicated engineering (pipes, ducts, pumps, valves, cable trays), the hidden complexity that keeps the simple surface simple. The design shows the split between the two worlds. Bonus: the lettering and the graphic are separate pieces, so the logo can be drawn in gradually (the mark first, then the title, or the reverse) on the title screen, the itch.io page and the trailer.

## What every variant must do

- The title reads THE POOL, spelt exactly so, in capitals or title case; no other words, no tagline.
- One clear dividing line, the water's surface, splits the design into two halves: above it calm, clean and sparse (flat water, a lane line, a tile edge, plenty of space); below it dense, intricate and mechanical (pipework with flanges and elbows, valves, pump volutes, ducts, cable trays, gauges), drawn as an engineer's cutaway.
- The contrast is the point: the top half simple enough to read at a glance; the bottom half rewarding a long look, but still orderly, as real plant is, not chaos.
- Flat vector style, a limited palette (one pool blue or teal, one deep navy or charcoal, white, at most one accent such as safety yellow or pipe-code green), no gradients except an optional single soft one on the water, no photographic texture, no drop shadows.
- Works at small sizes (a 64 px icon of the mark alone) and large (a 4K title card).
- An adult, slightly dry, engineering tone: no cartoon characters, swimmers, mascots, splashes, rubber rings or beach imagery.
- Delivered as separate layers: (1) the graphic alone, without any lettering; (2) the lettering alone, on a transparent background; (3) the two combined. The lettering must sit on the graphic so it can fade or wipe in independently, without anything of the graphic being hidden only behind the text.

## The ten variants

1. **The cutaway square.** A square badge cut by a horizontal waterline a third of the way down: above, flat blue water and one lane rope; below, a dense isometric tangle of pipes, valves and a pump, in navy line work. THE POOL set in a clean geometric sans above the waterline, in the calm half.
2. **The iceberg.** A calm, rectangular pool seen from the side, small at the top; beneath it the plant room spreads out three times wider and deeper, like an iceberg's hidden mass, in technical line drawing. Lettering across the surface line, the waterline running through the middle of the letters' height.
3. **Letters as the pool.** THE POOL in a wide, heavy sans whose letterforms are filled with flat water at the top; from the bottom of each letter, pipes drop down and join into a manifold under the word, ending in one pump. The graphic layer is the pipework and the water; the lettering layer is the letters' outlines.
4. **The reflection.** Like a mirror image in water: above the line, THE POOL in clean, simple type over a minimalist pool; below the line, its "reflection" is not the letters but a pipework schematic in the same shape, its runs tracing the letters' strokes.
5. **The circle.** A circular emblem split horizontally: the top half a plain disc of pool blue with a single white tile line; the bottom half a cross-section of a plant room, ducts and pipes packed into the semicircle like a watch movement. Lettering set in an arc above the circle, separable.
6. **Blueprint below.** The top half a flat colour illustration of a modern pool hall (white walls, blue water, a clock); the bottom half the same scene as an engineering blueprint, white lines on blueprint blue, every pipe, valve and pump drawn with dimension lines and tags. Lettering in a stencil-like engineering face along the dividing line.
7. **The drain.** Minimal: a flat pool surface, a single floor grille at its centre, and through it, opening downward like a funnel, an ever more intricate spread of pipes, pumps and filters. Lettering above, sparse and centred.
8. **Tiles to circuits.** The top half a grid of plain white pool tiles with one blue lane line; at the waterline the grid lines turn into pipe runs and cable trays that branch, bend and multiply in the bottom half, like a circuit board drawn as plumbing. Lettering in a single weight sans across the tiles.
9. **The section drawing.** A long horizontal format: an architect's section through the building. Above ground, a simple pool and a flat roof, drawn in two lines; below the deck, the basement plant room in full detail, balance tank, filters, pumps and boiler, colour-coded pipework (BS 1710 colours). Lettering in the empty sky above the roof.
10. **Monogram.** A compact square mark for icons: a capital P whose bowl is a calm pool of water seen from above and whose stem descends as a vertical pipe into a cluster of valves and flanges below a waterline. The full title THE POOL set beside it, separable, for wide uses.

[WITH TEXT: The words "THE POOL" in a clean geometric sans-serif, spelt exactly, placed in the calm upper half.] [WITHOUT TEXT: No lettering or text of any kind.] Centred on a plain background, high contrast, crisp edges, suitable as a game logo.

Produce each variant twice, with and without the text, so the lettering can be drawn in separately.
