# 095: The bridge becomes the control room

Status: under way (2026-10-27): stage 1 done, the bridge a game room off the main corridor (`?room=bridge`), the old control room retired and kept by its URL (`?room=control_room`); then the bridge's look after the owner's brief below (2026-10-27); the first batch of panels wired (below); the live viewscreen (2026-10-27); next, the rest of the control room's boards. Written 2026-10-27 from the owner's request.

You are working on "The Pool", a browser game in TypeScript and Three.js simulating a leisure centre's pool plant (this repository). Read `CLAUDE.md` first, then missions [092](092-control-room-2010s.md) (the grand control room and its dev rooms dev45 and dev46) and [093](093-bridge-widget-kit.md) (the ui2d kit, the touch surfaces, dev47 and dev48's 69 panels reimagined), and [the Central Control research](../research/central-control-console.md). Markdown is one paragraph per line.

## The request

The owner, 2026-10-27, of dev48's reimagined panels: "These are cool. So do you think you can try and get some of them installed into the Star Trek Bridge dev room and try to wire them up now to the real world simulation model ... In fact, actually, why don't we wire this in as the real replacement control room? Let's back up the old control room, or just disconnect it, and we'll just call this room the bridge."

## The owner's rulings this carries

- The control room is replaced by **the bridge**: dev46's room (the 8 m stair, the heptagon, the stage, the starship finish) with the 24th-century touch panels, wired to the one world state. The touch screens are the bridge's own kind of interface (the archetype in `doc/design/control-interface-archetypes.md`); this is the owner's ruling for this room, the plant rooms keeping their single-purpose instruments.
- The old control room is kept, disconnected: no longer reached from the corridor, its layout and models kept (retired, as `retired-rooms.ts` keeps rooms, or reachable as a dev room by URL), so it can be restored.

## The work

1. **The room:** a game room `bridge`, built from dev46's geometry and finish (the shared builder), reached from the main corridor's door where `control_room` was (`CORRIDOR_DOORS` in `main-corridor.ts`, the room links, titles and signs), its stair hall's door being that doorway. The old `control_room` retired: its door gone from the corridor, its room kept as above. Saves: if anything in the world names the control room (the CCTV, alarms printer, mimics' copies), carry it over or migrate (`WORLD_VERSION` and a migration only if the world's shape changes).
2. **The panels, wired:** the control room's panels and mimics reimagined from dev48's specifications (`parody-specs.ts`), each now reading and acting on the world exactly as its original does (the same world reads and the same actions the original's view uses: the mimics' OPEN and SHUT and START and STOP, the annunciator's ACCEPT and RESET, the readings, the stocks), placed round the bridge's walls and at its system stations (circulation and filtration, chemical dosing, heating, air handling, power, building). Local-only controls (`CLAUDE.md`: mode selectors, trip resets) shown but refused here (a deny bleep and a "LOCAL CONTROL ONLY" message), as the control room's boards refuse them now.
3. **Central Control,** the operator's console, from the research's list as far as the model goes today: the time and whether open, water quality per pool, bathers per pool against the limit, the alarm summary with ACCEPT, the fire alarm's state, pumps and filters, power, chemical stocks; the items not yet modelled (pool emergency alarm, dosing stop, PA) shown as placeholders and listed in `TODO.md`.
4. **The main viewscreen:** a live overview from the world (an animated schematic of the main pool circuit, or the alarm list when an alarm is up) for now; the full modes are mission 093's stage 2.
5. **Checks:** the game's tests (world-in-model: every panel bound to the world or decorative), the layout tests, the corridor door and room handover, frame time in the room (redraw only on change), screenshots of the room and of a panel before and after a command that changes the world (a pump started from the bridge, seen running in its plant room).

## The bridge's look (the owner, 2026-10-27)

The owner, while it was being built: "light the room like the Star Trek bridge. That means we want cove lighting, big sweeping curves ... soft chairs ... a sort of beige or peach coloured carpet ... how much realism we can add"; then "I have a feeling there's two layers of cove lighting, one pointing inwards to the dome and one pointing outwards to the edges of the room ... there's very little direct lighting on the Enterprise Bridge ... soften some of the corners because it's also a very rounded space." After the 1987 bridge set (Herman Zimmerman, Andrew Probert; references: [Ex Astris Scientia on the Enterprise-D bridge](https://www.ex-astris-scientia.org/inconsistencies/enterprise-d-bridge.htm), [WhatCulture, 10 secrets of the TNG main bridge](https://whatculture.com/tv/star-trek-10-secrets-of-the-next-generation-main-bridge)), as a style cue only, in the game's own design: no names, logos, LCARS artwork or fonts.

- **Lighting:** soft, warm, even and mostly indirect: a lit oval dome over the command area, glossy black panels round it; two concentric coves round the dome, the inner washing up into it, the outer, in a trough further out, washing the ceiling outward; six ribs fanning forward to the big wall, a cove along each; a cove along every wall's top; a light strip over the aft consoles; a row of small chaser lights along the viewscreen's foot running out from the middle. Few real lights (one in the dome, a few just under the ceiling at the coves, a hemisphere fill); no downlights or spots.
- **Curves:** the stage's front a horseshoe (an arc curving 1.8 m back in the middle round the command area), its parapet a beige plinth with a light oak cap rail with black and silver lines, the ramps either side curving with it, the steps in its middle; a large rounded fillet in every corner of the heptagon, floor to ceiling.
- **Furnishings:** upholstered chairs (fabric, not leather): the operator's larger, with padded arms and small black insets at their fronts for Central Control's armrest panels; a softer one either side; smaller, taller ones at the stations; the conference table's to match.
- **Colours:** a warm peach-beige carpet with a fine tuft, a darker ring round the command area, burgundy carpet on the stage's face (the low wall behind the command area); beige walls and ribs; light oak with a strong grain.
- Built in `views/bridge-dressing.ts`, used by the shared builder for dev46 and the game's bridge. Not yet: rounded edges on the EPCOT desk and the stations, grey fabric in door alcoves (the bridge has none on its level), a coved wall-to-ceiling junction beyond the wall-top cove.

## Touch panels: redraw only on change (2026-10-27)

Mission 094's benchmark found every touch panel re-uploading its whole canvas about 60 times a second. Before any panel went onto the bridge the touch surface's host was changed to repaint and upload only when its `ui2d` tree is dirty, its own animations capped at 10 Hz by the clock (`PAINT_HZ`). Measured with the component benchmark (`bench.html?bench=components&only=^exhibit:dev4[78]:...`): dev47's sidebar frame 59.8 uploads/s and 68.9 M texels/s before, 9.2 and 10.6 M after; dev48's CR-CP2 59.9 and 35.5 M before, 9.2 and 5.4 M after; dev48's PL2-CP1 47.4 and 23.8 M before, 9.2 and 4.6 M after (all three animate on their trials); SR-IS3, still, 0 before and after.

## The stations' schematics (the owner, 2026-10-27)

The owner: "design some schematic displays in the LCARS style that would suit each of the stations, and make it so that there could be more different designs than we have placeholders. So periodically, the schematic switches to a different representation." Then, of dev48's OLYMPIC HYDRODYNAMIC OVERVIEW (CR-MP1): "I'd love to see schematics for all of them in that similar style that we could bring up on the individual consoles"; and all of them in the touch-screen galleries, each labelled.

Built:

- **In `src/ui2d/schematic.ts`** (generic, no game or Three.js; tested in `schematic.test.ts`): `FlowNetwork` (machines as glyphs: pump, valve, tank, filter, boiler, fan, breaker, pool, heat exchanger, meter, UV, source, junction; pipes as rounded bars with dashes or chevrons moving at a speed that follows each pipe's rate), `Vessel` (a vessel cut away, its level, marks, bubbles while active, a heat column's bands), `RingGauge` and `Grid` (ring clusters), `SensorSweep` (a scanning bar down a list, paging a long one), `StatusGrid` (a named matrix of state cells), `SequenceStrip` (steps, the current one filling), `Reticle` (a targeting scope of two readings and their zone), `Rotator` (designs in turn every `period` s, default 18, a wipe as each comes up, a page indicator, a cycle subset, `select` to hold one, only the shown design ticked) and `SchematicBrowser` (a sidebar frame listing the designs a page at a time with MORE, a press bringing one up and holding it, CYCLE to resume). Self-animation steps at most 10 Hz (`ANIMATION_HZ`); everything else repaints only on a changed value.
- **The hydrodynamic overviews** (`logic/schematic-overviews.ts`): in the house style of CR-MP1 (the circuit round a loop or along a line, the moving water, a column of read-outs): LOUNGE, OLYMPIC, JACUZZI and FOOTBATH HYDRODYNAMIC OVERVIEW; the heating ring; the gas train; hypochlorite and acid distribution; air handling; power distribution; backwash and drainage; make-up water. Each stage is its room's model's nodes by id (`SCHEMATICS`, the Olympic's `olympicPlumbing()`), and the test traces every step through the model's links (through headers only), so the drawing is the model's topology; labels are derived from the node ids or the motors' tags. The Olympic's return from the pool to its tank (the hall's overflow channels) and the air's extract are not in their rooms' models and are said so (`returnOutside`). The world gives the heat the pools take, not which boiler gives it: the screens stage the six boilers on that demand at 250 kW each (`BOILER_STAGE_KW`), and say so.
- **The stations' designs** (`logic/station-schematics.ts`, at least three each, more than their screens): FLOW NAVIGATOR (Olympic, lounge, jacuzzi and footbath overviews; filter bank matrix with the Wash-O-Tron's programme strip and each filter's next wash; turnover rings); BOILER HELMSMAN (heating ring; warp core heat column with the pools' temperatures; gas train; boiler cascade); CHIEF ELECTRICAL ENGINEER (power distribution; feeder matrix; load rings of the motors' currents, site load and battery; isolation sweep of the isolators and lighting ways); DISINFECTION TACTICAL (chlorine and pH targeting reticles per pool; hypochlorite and acid distribution; dosing pump sweep; residual rings); LIFE SUPPORT OFFICER (air handling; AHU matrix; atmosphere rings; air filter scan); SECURITY CHIEF (fire and gas defence grid; gas detection sweep; compartments by lighting way; alarm sweep); WATER SCIENCE OFFICER (trend wall; water quality matrix; combined chlorine and clarity rings; every reading swept); DRAINAGE OFFICER (backwash and drainage; reservoirs; make-up water; wash sequence with the sewer consent); LOADING BAY CHIEF (bulk stores with days left; dock status; stock projection and the meters); OPERATIONS OFFICER (both distributions; day tanks; transfer lines); CENTRAL CONTROL (site status; bather load; alarm sweep; the day; the Olympic); MAIN VIEWSCREEN (Olympic, lounge, heating and power overviews; the plant at a glance; alarm sweep). All read the world; nothing in them is simulated. The world has no doors or CCTV state yet, so the Security Chief's designs are fire, gas, lighting and alarms.
- **Where to see them:** dev48 (`?room=dev48`), after its 69 reimagined panels: every overview, every other design, the rotator and the browser, each on its own screen and plaque; dev49 (`?room=dev49`), each station's browser, labelled, set apart in time.

### Mounting them (the API)

In `src/toolkit/gallery/views/station-schematics.ts`:

- `createStationSchematic(kit, station, { w, h }, context, { period?, offset?, cycle?, pixelsPerMetre? }): LiveExhibit` — a station's browser as a touch surface, its glass `w × h` metres about its origin facing +z, reading `context()` (a `{ world, reading }`, as the bridge's own `context`, `reading = plantReadingOf(world, 'pool_02')`) twice a second; uses go to its browser (a design from the list, MORE, CYCLE, the indicator). `station` is one of `STATION_NAMES` (`logic/station-schematics.ts`; the officers' names as the bridge's `DUTIES` has them, plus CENTRAL CONTROL and MAIN VIEWSCREEN; `stationNamed(name)` checks one).
- `stationSchematic(station, options)` — the same as a bare `ui2d` tree with `update(context, seconds)`, to put in any touch surface of one's own (a screen among others on one frame).
- `designScreen(designs, { mode: 'single' | 'rotator' | 'browser', title, … })` and `createSchematicSurface(kit, screen, name, size, context)` — any list of designs (`STATION_DESIGNS[...]`, `{ kind: 'overview', overview: id }` for any of `OVERVIEWS`), alone, turning, or browsable.
- Set screens that start together apart with `offset` (seconds into the first turn), so a row of them does not turn at once.
