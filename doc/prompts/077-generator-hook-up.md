# 077: The generator hook-up: cabling the power lorry to the connection cabinet

Status: incomplete, for planning (opened 2026-10-26 with the owner, from [073](073-loading-dock-layout.md)). Not ready to start: the questions below are to be settled with the owner first.

## The idea

When the generator lorry stands in the right lane, its containerised set is cabled to the heavy connection cabinet's terminals (three phases and earth), and the building is changed over to it. External power is delivered by a containerised generator on a lorry (the owner, 2026-10-26), so this is how the game simulates it.

## To settle

- The terminals: single-pole connectors of the Powerlock kind, L1 L2 L3 and earth, and whether there is a neutral.
- The changeover: where it is (the main switchboard?), manual or automatic, and its interlocks so mains and generator are never paralleled.
- The generator itself: its controller, starting it, its fuel, its load, what is heard.
- What the building runs on generator, and what it sheds.
