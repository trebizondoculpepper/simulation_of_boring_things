# Midjourney prompts: "Never again", the fictional 1997 chlorine release

Prompts for the photographs of the museum's "Never again" hall ([107i](../107i-never-again.md)), written 2026-10-28. The incident is fictional: a spring morning in 1997 at an unnamed British municipal leisure centre, where acid was delivered into the sodium hypochlorite tank. The six chapters follow 107i's timeline.

Rules for every image: no real place, venue, company or person; no readable real brand names or logos (signage and liveries generic or blank); people are anonymous and unidentifiable (backs turned, masks, distance, blur); nobody shown injured or dead, as nobody died; British details (right-hand-drive vehicles, number plates blurred or blank, 1990s UK fire service kit). The look is a regional newspaper's press photograph of 1997: 35 mm film, slight grain, flash or flat overcast light, a little soft.

Common suffix, appended to each prompt below:

```
1997 British regional newspaper press photograph, 35mm film, Kodak Tri-X or faded Fujicolor, visible grain, slightly soft focus, documentary, no text, no logos, anonymous unidentifiable people --ar 3:2 --style raw --v 7
```

For the black-and-white front-page versions, add `black and white` before the suffix; for the colour inside-page versions, `faded 1990s colour print`.

## Chapter 1: The delivery, 07:42

```
an unmarked chemical road tanker, right-hand drive, parked at the loading bay of a 1970s concrete municipal leisure centre at dawn, grey English drizzle, the steel fill-point recess in the brick wall standing open, no staff in sight, wet tarmac, sodium streetlights still on
```

## Chapter 2: The mix-up, 07:51

```
close-up of a gloved hand in a heavy rubber glove coupling a black chemical hose to one of two identical steel camlock fittings side by side in an open recess, paper labels above them sun-faded and blank, harsh camera flash, shallow depth of field, ominous ordinary moment
```

## Chapter 3: The gas, 07:58

```
inside a cramped swimming pool plant room in 1997, large plastic chemical tanks behind a concrete bund, a faint pale yellow-green haze drifting from a tank vent pipe, a wall-mounted gas detector with its cable hanging loose and unplugged, pipes and valves, fluorescent tube light, no people
```

## Chapter 4: The evacuation, 08:05

```
the car park of a British leisure centre on a cold spring morning, early swimmers wrapped in silver foil blankets and towels standing in small groups seen from behind, firefighters in bulky gas-tight chemical protection suits and breathing apparatus at the entrance, 1990s British fire engines and an ambulance, blue lights reflected on wet tarmac, cordon tape
```

## Chapter 5: The inquiry, weeks later

```
the locked glass entrance doors of a closed 1970s municipal swimming baths, a handwritten "closed until further notice" notice taped inside (the words blurred and illegible), an empty reception and turnstiles behind the glass, dead leaves on the step, overcast, melancholy
```

Variant, the inspectors:

```
two anonymous inspectors in anoraks with clipboards, seen from behind, examining an open steel chemical fill recess on the outside wall of a leisure centre, a measuring tape and a camera, overcast daylight
```

## Chapter 6: The changes

```
a new, clean, lockable steel chemical delivery cabinet on the brick wall of a British leisure centre, two separate compartments with different coloured doors, two different kinds of hose coupling, bright hazard pictogram labels, a padlock, fresh paint, bright daylight, hopeful
```
