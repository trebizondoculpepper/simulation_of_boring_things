# The pool rooms: a first layout

A small piece of the endless pool rooms we imagine (`two-realms.md`), laid out bare to judge its shape before anything is dressed. The plan is pure, tested data in `src/game/poolrooms-plan.ts`; the blockout that draws it is `src/rendering/poolrooms-world.ts`, at `?room=poolrooms`. It stands beside the current leisure centre (`facility-plan.ts`, which follows a real building), not in place of it, until the owner decides.

## What the pool rooms are

The pool rooms are the plant's outputs: the water's level, its temperature, its cleanliness. They need no machinery and no plausible architecture, only spaces to show those few things in. Pool rooms never look architecturally feasible, so these do not try: a lane that leaves the main pool through an arch and runs sixty metres down a low corridor to a blank wall; columns rising from the main pool's water into an eighteen-metre ceiling; a river that runs uphill beside its ramp; terraces of ever-shallower pools above the main hall; a rotunda with a round jacuzzi, reached by a long narrow stair; a slide that leaves the spa through its wall, coils through the dark outside, and comes in through the grotto's wall into a splash pool four metres below the halls.

Within the pool rooms, geometry is consistent: no two zones overlap in plan, so every point has one floor, and every opening joins two zones whose floors meet there. Only the doors out of the pool rooms are free of it.

Four levels (−4, 0, 4 and 9 m) give the verticality: up by stairs from the hall to the terraces and the spa, down by the slide to the grotto, and back up by the river's ramp, a loop.

## Arches

Every opening between zones is an arch, as pool rooms have: round-headed where the wall is tall enough, flattened to half an ellipse where a wide opening is in a low wall (the lane, the gallery). Down each side of the main pool runs an arcade, its arches carried on the hall's columns. Every arch is softly lit from below: a small uplighter at each of its feet washes a warm glow up its jambs, fading before the spring of its head.

## Skylights, windows and lamps

Daylight comes in by skylights: six square lanterns over the main hall, clear of its columns, small squares down the footbath gallery, two over the terraces, a round oculus over the spa's jacuzzi, and four great ones, the quadrants of its ceiling, over the winding river. Windows look out onto bright, hazy nothing: a clerestory high round the main hall, low wide windows on the terraces (low enough for a child), one tall window in the spa, and a row along the river room's east wall. Lamps: frosted globes hanging on long rods down the main hall, sconces at a steady rhythm all the way down the endless lane, globes on the gallery's ceiling, a sconce on each stair, globes on posts along the river's banks, and in the grotto, which has no daylight, warm lamps on its walls that are the only light there.

## Sunlight

The sun is one light, from the south-west and 55° up, casting shadows across the whole of the pool rooms: it reaches a floor only where a skylight or a window lets it through, and lands offset from the opening, as slanting sun does, so the rooms are lit in patches with the shadows of their beams, columns, plants and furniture across them. The sky's softer light fills the rest. Nothing in the pool rooms moves, so their shadows are drawn once.

## Doors to the plant rooms

Every zone but its stairs has a plain door marked AUTHORISED PERSONNEL ONLY, as mundane as any service door. Using it takes the walker out of the pool rooms entirely, to a grey room standing far off in the void (for now, in place of that zone's plant room), with a door marked POOL that brings them back through the door they came by. Eight zones, eight doors, eight grey rooms.

## Water treatment display boards

Every swimming pool has its water treatment display board on its deck, as UK pools post their test results: the pool's name, free and combined chlorine, pH, temperature and the time of the last test. The footbaths have none. For now the boards show the readings of a pool kept well; once the pools are blocks in the plant model (`plant-model.md`), each board shows its own pool's state, so that something going wrong in the plant rooms shows here first.

## Ideas for more zones

Not yet built, for the owner to choose from: a wave pool whose waves arrive from a horizon; a diving pool with a tower climbing through two levels; a flooded stair, walked down into the water; a pool seen from below through a glass floor; cascading infinity pools down a hillside of tile; a dark tiled tunnel lit only from the water; a sunken lounge; a toddlers' pool in a room too low to stand in; a tall, thin room with a single plunge pool at its foot; a lazy river that passes through every zone (the winding river is a first step: it already comes from somewhere and goes somewhere else).
