# Pool fittings: real products to model on

Researched 2026-10-20 for dev12 and the fittings in dev11 (`src/toolkit/components/pool/views/pool-fittings.ts`), which were first modelled generically. Each fitting is to be remodelled on the product below, under a brand of the game's own (the Encabulator's makers make anything; add a pool specialist only where none fits). Dimensions are from the manufacturers' catalogues and drawings; where a source gave none, it says so. Not yet acted on: the owner called the pool done for now. The source PDFs were downloaded to the session scratchpad, not the repository.

The dev12 models already match some of this (a 500 mm square drain cover, 8 mm slots, a 245 mm grating); the differences to make are mainly the faces: the drain's slot field and screws, the inlet's teardrop slots, the eyeball's cross grille, the vacuum plug's star recess, the gully's ring of holes, and the light's 250–295 mm faceplate.
