# Plant products: pump room and balance tank room

Researched 2026-10-20 for the equipment development rooms `dev_pumps` (`src/toolkit/gallery/rooms/plant/pumps.ts`, models in `src/toolkit/components/plant/views/pumps-plant.ts`) and `dev_balance` (`balance.ts`, models in `balance-plant.ts`). Each machine is modelled on the product below, under a maker of the game's own (Grundflow, Lidoluxe, Splashonomics, Torvald, Neumann Dynamik, Gorskiy, Kessler-Moody, Pepperdyne). Where a source gave no dimension, the model's is an estimate from the product's photographs and its class, and is marked so here.
