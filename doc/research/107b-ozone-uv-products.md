# Hall 2's ozone and UV: products and numbers (107b)

Researched 2026-10-28 for PLANTROOMS Hall 2 ("Clean water"), building on `doc/research/ozone-plant.md`, which holds the sourced background (PWTAG's layout, DIN 19643's design values, the ProMinent and Ozonia datasheets). This note records only what Hall 2 uses. Figures marked _indicative_ are from general trade knowledge and not verified against a source here; _derived_ are worked from the hall's model (`src/game/plant/museum-2-clean-water.ts`).

## The duty

The hall circulates about 184 m³/h (_derived_, dev51's pump and filter with the treatment train's losses). At PWTAG's 1 g of ozone per m³ treated (`ozone-plant.md` §1) that needs about 185 g/h of ozone, and at least two minutes' contact, so a contact tank of at least 6.1 m³.

## Conflicts and gaps

- One 120 m³/h Quantum would not take the hall's 184 m³/h; a real plant would fit a larger chamber or two in parallel. The hall shows one unit (_indicative_ sizing).
- The UV sensor's reading in W/m² and the warm-up time are not sourced; no lamp ageing or failure is modelled (as `uv.ts`).
- The carbon's life in a pool is still unknown (`ozone-plant.md`); the hall never exhausts it.
- No UK pool source gives ozone-monitor setpoints; 0.1 / 0.2 ppm stays inferred, now with a US pool code supporting 0.1 ppm.
