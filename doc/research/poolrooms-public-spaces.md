# Public pool visual references

The visual direction combines the unusually quiet, repeated white ceramic surfaces and overhead luminaires shown in [POOLS screenshots](https://blog.playstation.com/2025/11/21/pools-brings-liminal-terror-to-ps5-and-ps-vr2-on-nov-25/) with ordinary operational details from real leisure centres. The game reference is a mood reference, not an architectural drawing.

[Badeanstalten Spanien's changing areas](https://vigirbyenpuls.dk/svoemmehaller/badeanstalten-spanien/omklaedning-og-opholdsrum) show tiled walls, locker banks, benches and modest fixtures. [Finnøy swimming pool](https://www.stavanger.kommune.no/en/kultur-og-fritid/swimming-pools/finnoy-swimming-pool/) gives another practical changing room reference. [Sport England's swimming pool guidance](https://www.sportengland.org/media/4187/swimming-pools-dgn-2013.pdf) informed the mix of cubicles, lockers, benches and pre swim showers.

The modeled basins use a pale coping, a narrow grating over a dark perimeter channel, and visible floor outlet grilles. These are visual approximations of commercial [overflow drainage components](https://www.waterstarpools.com/category/overflow-drainage), not construction specifications. The pools remain empty so the tiled walls, lane lines and outlets can be inspected.
